# Suggested Papers — Ready-to-Read Index

This folder is the reading queue for papers that have been collected but not
yet studied closely enough to become research topics.

Status meanings:

- **To read:** collected, but not yet read with understanding.
- **Reading:** active reading notes are being developed.
- **Understood:** sufficiently understood to consider creating a structured
  note under `topics/`.
- **Topic created:** the resulting concept note has been published under
  `topics/` and linked here.

Collecting a paper does not imply endorsement, mastery, or adoption of its
claims. Before moving a paper into a topic, read it critically and verify the
concepts against its cited foundations where necessary.

### Reading Set: Photonic QEC Foundations

These papers were collected on **2026-08-13** while preparing for the TU
Delft/QuTech QuBriC PhD application on quantum error correction for photonics.

Suggested first pass:

1. Spend 35 minutes on the abstract, introduction, overview figure, and
   photonic error model in Bartolucci et al.
2. Spend 20 minutes on the abstract, popular summary/introduction, and
   architecture-comparison figure in Bombin et al.
3. Spend five minutes writing definitions of resource state, fusion, erasure,
   decoder, threshold, and ZX calculus in your own words.

Do not create a public topic merely because the papers were downloaded. Create
one only after the central ideas can be explained accurately and independently.
