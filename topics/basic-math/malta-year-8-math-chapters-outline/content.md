## Section 2 — Key Equations

### 1. Chapter 1: Directed Numbers

- Number line: $-5 < -2 < 0 < 3$
- Adding:
  - $-7 + 2 = -5$
  - $-3 + 8 = 5$
- Subtracting past zero: $4 - 9 = -5$
- Subtracting a negative:
  - $6 - (-2) = 6 + 2$
  - $6 + 2 = 8$
- Signs:
  - $(-3)\times(-4) = 12$
  - $(-3)\times4 = -12$
  - $-20\div5 = -4$

**Try:** $-6 + 10 - 9$

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

$$
\begin{aligned} -6 + 10 - 9 &= 4 - 9 \\ &= \boxed{-5} \end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### 2. Chapter 2: Number Work

- BIDMAS, multiply first:
  - $3 + 4\times2 = 3 + 8$
  - $3 + 8 = 11$
- Powers:
  - $2^3 = 2\times2\times2$
  - $2\times2\times2 = 8$
- Roots:
  - $\sqrt{49} = 7$
  - $\sqrt[3]{27} = 3$
- Prime factors: $24 = 2^3\times3$
- HCF and LCM:
  - $\text{HCF}(12, 18) = 6$
  - $\text{LCM}(4, 6) = 12$
- Rounding: $3.47 \approx 3.5$ (1 decimal place)

**Try:** $20 - 3\times4$

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

Multiply first:

$$
\begin{aligned} 20 - 3\times4 &= 20 - 12 \\ &= \boxed{8} \end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### 3. Chapter 3: Angles

- Straight line: $a + b = 180^\circ$
- Around a point: $a + b + c = 360^\circ$
- Vertically opposite angles are equal
- Triangle: $a + b + c = 180^\circ$
- Exterior angle = sum of the two opposite inside angles
- Parallel lines:
  - corresponding angles (F) are equal
  - alternate angles (Z) are equal
  - co-interior angles (C) add up to $180^\circ$

- **Try:** two angles on a straight line; one is $115^\circ$.
- Find the other.

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

$$
180^\circ - 115^\circ = \boxed{65^\circ}
$$

</details>

### 4. Chapter 4: Fractions

- Same denominator: $\frac{2}{7} + \frac{3}{7} = \frac{5}{7}$
- Common denominator:
  - $\frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{8}{12} + \frac{3}{12}$
  - $\frac{8}{12} + \frac{3}{12} = \frac{11}{12}$
- Multiply:
  - $\frac{2}{3}\times\frac{3}{5} = \frac{6}{15}$
  - $\frac{6}{15} = \frac{2}{5}$
- Divide — multiply by the flipped fraction:
  - $\frac{3}{4}\div\frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{4}\times\frac{2}{1}$
  - $\frac{3}{4}\times\frac{2}{1} = \frac{3}{2}$
- Mixed number: $2\tfrac{1}{3} = \frac{7}{3}$
- Fraction of an amount: $\frac{3}{4}$ of $20 = 15$

**Try:** $\frac{2}{5}$ of €30

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

$$
\begin{aligned} 30\div5 &= 6 \\ 6\times2 &= \boxed{12} \end{aligned}
$$\n\nSo $\frac{2}{5}$ of €30 is €12.

</details>

### 5. Chapter 5: Decimals

- Line up the decimal points: $4.5 + 0.38 = 4.88$
- $\times10$ moves every digit one place left: $0.36\times10 = 3.6$
- Multiply: $0.2\times0.3 = 0.06$
- Fractions as decimals:
  - $\frac{1}{4} = 0.25$
  - $\frac{1}{3} = 0.333\ldots$
- Rounding: $2.68 \approx 2.7$ (1 decimal place)

**Try:** €3.75 + €1.40

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

$$
3.75 + 1.40 = \boxed{5.15}
$$\n\nThe total is €5.15.

</details>

### 6. Chapter 6: Percentages

- $x\%$ of $A = \frac{x}{100}\times A$
- Quick percentages:
  - $10\%$: divide by 10
  - $50\%$: halve
- One value, three ways:
  - $25\% = \frac{1}{4}$
  - $\frac{1}{4} = 0.25$
- Increase by $p\%$: new amount $= A\times\left(1 + \frac{p}{100}\right)$
- Percentage change $= \frac{\text{change}}{\text{original}}\times100\%$

**Try:** $15\%$ of €40

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

$$
\begin{aligned} 10\% &= 4 \\ 5\% &= 2 \\ 15\% &= \boxed{6} \end{aligned}
$$\n\nSo $15\%$ of €40 is €6.

</details>

### 7. Chapter 7: Ratio

- Simplify: $12 : 8 = 3 : 2$
- Share $T$ in the ratio $a : b$: one part $= \frac{T}{a + b}$
- Unitary method: 3 for €6, so 1 for €2
- Map scale $1 : 50\,000$: 1 cm on the map is 500 m

**Try:** share €30 in the ratio $2 : 3$

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

$$
\begin{aligned} 2 + 3 &= 5 \text{ parts} \\ 30\div5 &= 6 \text{ per part} \\ 2\times6 &= \boxed{12} \\ 3\times6 &= \boxed{18} \end{aligned}
$$

The shares are €12 and €18.

</details>

### 8. Chapter 8: Algebra

- Like terms: $3x + 2x = 5x$
- Expand: $3(x + 4) = 3x + 12$
- Substitute $x = 5$:
  - $2x + 1 = 2\times5 + 1$
  - $2\times5 + 1 = 11$
- Solve:
  - $2x + 5 = 17$
  - $2x = 12$
  - $x = 6$
- Unknowns on both sides:
  - $5x - 3 = 2x + 9$
  - $3x = 12$
  - $x = 4$

**Try:** solve $3x - 4 = 11$

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

$$
\begin{aligned} 3x &= 15 \\ x &= \boxed{5} \end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### 9. Chapter 9: Sequences

- Term-to-term rule: $5, 8, 11, 14, \ldots$ (add 3)
- $n\text{th}$ term of that sequence: $3n + 2$
- $n\text{th}$ term $= d\,n + (\text{first term} - d)$, where $d$ is the difference

**Try:** the 10th term of $3n + 2$

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

$$
3\times10 + 2 = \boxed{32}
$$

</details>

### 10. Chapter 10: Circles

- Diameter $d = 2r$
- Circumference:
  - $C = \pi d$
  - $C = 2\pi r$
- Area $A = \pi r^2$
- $\pi \approx 3.14$

**Try:** the circumference of a circle with radius 5 cm

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

$$
\begin{aligned} C &= 2\times\pi\times5 \\ &= 10\pi \\ &\approx \boxed{31.4 \text{ cm}} \end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### 11. Chapter 11: Perimeter & Area

- Perimeter = all the sides added
- Rectangle: $A = l\times w$
- Triangle: $A = \frac{1}{2}bh$
- Parallelogram: $A = bh$
- Trapezium: $A = \frac{1}{2}(a + b)h$

**Try:** the area of a triangle with base 8 cm and height 5 cm

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

$$
\begin{aligned} A &= \tfrac{1}{2}\times8\times5 \\ &= \boxed{20 \text{ cm}^2} \end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### 12. Chapter 12: Surface Area & Nets

- Cube with edge $a$: $SA = 6a^2$
- Cuboid: $SA = 2(lw + lh + wh)$
- Cuboid volume: $V = lwh$
- The net of a cuboid has 6 rectangles

**Try:** the surface area of a cube with edges of 3 cm

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

$$
\begin{aligned} 6\times3^2 &= 6\times9 \\ &= \boxed{54 \text{ cm}^2} \end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### 13. Chapter 13: Graphs

- A point $(x, y)$: across first, then up
- Straight line: $y = mx + c$
- Gradient:
  - $m = \frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}}$
  - $m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$
- $c$ is where the line crosses the $y$-axis

**Try:** $y = 2x + 1$ when $x = 3$

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

$$
\begin{aligned} y &= 2\times3 + 1 \\ &= \boxed{7} \end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### 14. Chapter 14: Statistics

- Mean $= \frac{\text{sum of the values}}{\text{number of values}}$
- Median: the middle value, once in order
- Mode: the most common value
- Range = largest − smallest
- Pie chart angle $= \frac{\text{frequency}}{\text{total}}\times360^\circ$

**Try:** the mean of $4, 7, 7, 10$

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{4 + 7 + 7 + 10}{4} &= \frac{28}{4} \\ &= \boxed{7} \end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### 15. Chapter 15: Probability

- $P(\text{event}) = \frac{\text{favourable outcomes}}{\text{all outcomes}}$
- $0 \le P \le 1$
- $P(\text{not } A) = 1 - P(A)$
- The probabilities of all the outcomes add up to 1

**Try:** the probability of an even number on a fair dice

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

Even numbers 2, 4, 6:

$$
\begin{aligned} P &= \frac{3}{6} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{1}{2}} \end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### 16. Chapter 16: Constructions

- Tools: ruler, compasses, protractor
- A triangle from three sides: the two shorter sides together must be longer than the third ($a + b > c$)
- An angle bisector splits $\theta$ into two angles of $\frac{\theta}{2}$
- A perpendicular bisector crosses a line at $90^\circ$ through its midpoint

**Try:** can sides of 3 cm, 4 cm and 8 cm make a triangle?

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

$$
\begin{aligned} 3 + 4 &= 7 < 8 \\ & \boxed{\text{No}} \end{aligned}
$$

</details>

### 17. Chapter 17: Transformations

- Reflect in the $y$-axis: $(x, y) \to (-x, y)$
- Reflect in the $x$-axis: $(x, y) \to (x, -y)$
- Rotate $180^\circ$ about the origin: $(x, y) \to (-x, -y)$
- Translate by $\binom{a}{b}$: $(x, y) \to (x + a, y + b)$

**Try:** translate $(2, 5)$ by $\binom{3}{-4}$

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

$$
(2 + 3,\ 5 - 4) = \boxed{(5, 1)}
$$

</details>

### 18. Chapter 18: Real-life Problems

- Change = money paid − cost
- Unit price = cost ÷ quantity (the best buy has the lower unit price)
- Speed $= \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{time}}$
- Currency: euros $\times$ exchange rate
- Time: 09:45 to 11:20 is 1 h 35 min

- **Try:** two pastizzi and a drink cost €4.65; you pay with a €10 note.
- Find the change.

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

$$
10 - 4.65 = \boxed{5.35}
$$\n\nThe change is €5.35.

</details>

### 19. Chapter 19: Logo

- `FD 100` moves forward 100 steps
- `BK 50` moves back 50 steps
- `RT 90` and `LT 90` turn right or left by $90^\circ$
- Square: `REPEAT 4 [FD 50 RT 90]`
- Regular polygon with $n$ sides: turn $\frac{360^\circ}{n}$ at each corner
- The turns of a closed shape add up to $360^\circ$

**Try:** write the commands for a regular hexagon with sides of 50

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

Turn $360^\circ\div6 = 60^\circ$ at each corner:

$$
\boxed{\texttt{REPEAT 6 [FD 50 RT 60]}}
$$

</details>
