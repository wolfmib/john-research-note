# Malta Year 7 Math — Year 7 (Short) Unit 1 to Unit 3 Recap

Three questions for each unit. **Show your working before choosing A–D.**

---

## 1. Unit 1 · Place value

What is the value of the digit 7 in 3,742,105?

- **A.** 7,000
- **B.** 70,000
- **C.** 700,000
- **D.** 7,000,000

## 2. Unit 1 · LCM

Ferries to Comino leave every 20 minutes and boat trips leave every 30 minutes. Both leave at 9:00. When do they next leave together?

- **A.** 9:10
- **B.** 10:00
- **C.** 9:50
- **D.** 19:00

## 3. Unit 1 · Powers, decimals, negatives

Which statement is true?

- **A.** $2^3 = 6$
- **B.** $-8 > -3$
- **C.** $4^2 = 8$
- **D.** $0.07 = 7\%$

## 4. Unit 2 · BIDMAS

Calculate $36\div4\times3 - 2^2$.

- **A.** 23
- **B.** −1
- **C.** 25
- **D.** 9

## 5. Unit 2 · Fractions

Calculate $\frac34\times\frac29$.

- **A.** $\frac{5}{13}$
- **B.** $\frac{27}{8}$
- **C.** $\frac16$
- **D.** $\frac{5}{36}$

## 6. Unit 2 · Direct proportion

5 kg of oranges cost €6.50. What do 8 kg cost?

- **A.** €9.50
- **B.** €13.00
- **C.** €52.00
- **D.** €10.40

## 7. Unit 3 · Sequences

What is the nth term of 5, 8, 11, 14, …?

- **A.** $n + 3$
- **B.** $3n + 5$
- **C.** $3n + 2$
- **D.** $5n + 3$

## 8. Unit 3 · Brackets

Simplify $3(2x + 5) - 4x$.

- **A.** $2x + 5$
- **B.** $2x + 15$
- **C.** $10x + 15$
- **D.** $2x + 8$

## 9. Unit 3 · Equations

Solve $4x + 9 = 37$.

- **A.** $x = 7$
- **B.** $x = 11.5$
- **C.** $x = 28$
- **D.** $x = 112$

---

<details>
<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. C — Hundred-thousands column**

Reading from the left: 3 millions, 7 hundred-thousands, 4 ten-thousands, so the 7 is worth 700,000.

**2. B — LCM of 20 and 30**

Multiples of 30: 30, 60, … and 60 is the first that 20 divides, so they meet again 60 minutes later, at 10:00.

$$
\text{LCM}(20, 30) = 60
$$

9:50 adds the two times, and 19:00 uses $20\times30 = 600$ minutes — a common multiple, but not the lowest.

**3. D — Seven hundredths is 7%**

$0.07 = \frac{7}{100} = 7\%$. The others are false: $2^3 = 8$, $4^2 = 16$, and $-8 < -3$ because $-8$ is further left on the number line.

**4. A — Indices, then ÷ and × left to right**

$$
36\div4\times3 - 2^2 = 9\times3 - 4 = 27 - 4 = \boxed{23}
$$

−1 comes from doing $4\times3$ before the division; 25 treats $2^2$ as 2.

**5. C — Multiply tops and bottoms**

$$
\frac34\times\frac29 = \frac{6}{36} = \boxed{\frac16}
$$

$\frac{5}{13}$ adds tops and bottoms; $\frac{27}{8}$ is the answer to a division.

**6. D — Cost of 1 kg first**

$$
6.50\div5 = 1.30, \qquad 8\times1.30 = \boxed{10.40}
$$

So 8 kg cost €10.40. €9.50 adds €3 for 3 more kg instead of €1.30 per kg.

**7. C — Step 3, then adjust**

The step is 3, so the rule starts with $3n$. At $n = 1$, $3n = 3$, and the first term is 5, so add 2: $3n + 2$.

$3n + 5$ uses the first term instead of the adjustment; it gives 8 for $n = 1$.

**8. B — Multiply both terms in the bracket**

$$
6x + 15 - 4x = \boxed{2x + 15}
$$

$2x + 5$ forgets to multiply the 5 by 3.

**9. A — Subtract 9, then divide by 4**

$$
4x = 28 \quad\Rightarrow\quad x = 7
$$

Check: $4\times7 + 9 = 37$. 11.5 adds 9 instead of subtracting it; 28 stops before dividing by 4.

Order matters: compare place value from the left, do × and ÷ from left to right, and undo the operations of an equation in reverse order.

</details>
