# SAT Math 1 — Linear Equations in One Variable Quiz

Five questions on linear equations in one variable. **Show your working before you choose or type an answer.**

---

## 1. Find the expression, not x

$$
4x - 9 = 11
$$

What is the value of $12x - 27$?

- **A.** $5$
- **B.** $33$
- **C.** $60$
- **D.** $15$

## 2. Two fractions

$$
\frac{x + 4}{3} - \frac{x - 2}{5} = 2
$$

What is the value of $x$?

Answer: ______

## 3. Decimals

$$
0.35(x + 20) = 0.15x + 13
$$

What is the value of $x$?

- **A.** $100$
- **B.** $6$
- **C.** $40$
- **D.** $30$

## 4. How many solutions?

$$
3(2x - 4) - x = 5x - 12
$$

How many solutions does the equation have?

- **A.** Exactly one
- **B.** Exactly two
- **C.** Infinitely many
- **D.** No solution

## 5. Two phone plans

Plan A costs €18 a month plus €0.05 per text message.

Plan B costs €12 a month plus €0.08 per text message.

For how many text messages in a month do the two plans cost the same?

Answer: ______

---

<details>
<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. B — Multiply the whole equation by 3**

$12x - 27$ is exactly 3 times $4x - 9$:

$$
\begin{aligned} 12x - 27 &= 3(4x - 9) \\ &= 3 \cdot 11 \\ &= \boxed{33} \end{aligned}
$$

5 is the value of $x$, not of the expression.

**2. $2$ — Multiply by 15**

$$
\begin{aligned} 5(x + 4) - 3(x - 2) &= 30 \\ 5x + 20 - 3x + 6 &= 30 \\ 2x + 26 &= 30 \\ x &= \boxed{2} \end{aligned}
$$

The trap: $-3(x - 2) = -3x + 6$, not $-3x - 6$.

**3. D — Multiply by 100 to clear the decimals**

$$
\begin{aligned} 35(x + 20) &= 15x + 1300 \\ 35x + 700 &= 15x + 1300 \\ 20x &= 600 \\ x &= \boxed{30} \end{aligned}
$$

6 comes from stopping at $0.2x = 6$.

**4. C — Simplify the left side**

$$
\begin{aligned} 3(2x - 4) - x &= 6x - 12 - x \\ &= 5x - 12 \end{aligned}
$$

Both sides are $5x - 12$, so every $x$ works: **infinitely many** solutions.

**5. $200$ — Set the two costs equal**

$$
\begin{aligned} 18 + 0.05t &= 12 + 0.08t \\ 6 &= 0.03t \\ t &= \boxed{200} \end{aligned}
$$

Check: both plans cost €28 for 200 texts.

Clear fractions and decimals first, then compare the x-terms and the constants to see one, none or infinitely many solutions.

</details>
