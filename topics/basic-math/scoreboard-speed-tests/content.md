<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

**Two unit fractions, one line:**

$$
\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}=\frac{a+b}{ab}
$$

- Top: add the bottoms. Bottom: multiply them.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{4} &= \frac{3+4}{12} \\
&= \boxed{\frac{7}{12}}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Three fractions:** find the common bottom once, then add the tops.

- For $\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{5}$ the common bottom is $3 \times 4 \times 5 = 60$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{5} &= \frac{20}{60}+\frac{15}{60}+\frac{12}{60} \\
&= \boxed{\frac{47}{60}}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Same idea, common bottom 12:**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{4} &= \frac{6}{12}+\frac{4}{12}+\frac{3}{12} \\
&= \boxed{\frac{13}{12}}
\end{aligned}
$$

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

**x on both sides:** move the $x$ terms to the side with **more** $x$, so $x$ stays positive.

- In $3x+7=5x-9$, the right side has more $x$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x+7 &= 5x-9 \\
7+9 &= 5x-3x \\
16 &= 2x \\
x &= \boxed{8}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Check in 3 seconds:** put the answer back into both sides.

$$
\begin{aligned}
3(8)+7 &= 31 \\
5(8)-9 &= 31
\end{aligned}
$$

- Both sides match, so $x=8$ is right.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

**One fraction on each side:** cross-multiply first.

$$
\frac{A}{3}=\frac{B}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 2A=3B
$$

**Example:** $\dfrac{2x+1}{3}=\dfrac{3x-3}{2}$

$$
\begin{aligned}
2(2x+1) &= 3(3x-3) \\
4x+2 &= 9x-9 \\
2+9 &= 9x-4x \\
11 &= 5x \\
x &= \boxed{2.2}
\end{aligned}
$$

**A decimal answer is fine.** $x = \frac{11}{5} = 2.2$ is not a mistake.

$$
\begin{aligned}
3(3x-2) &= 4(x+5) \\
9x-6 &= 4x+20 \\
5x &= 26 \\
x &= \boxed{5.2}
\end{aligned}
$$

</details>
