# AP Precalculus — Simultaneous Equations Recap

**12 systems: 4 Easy, 4 Medium, 4 Hard.** Each has two equations and one pair $(x,\ y)$ that fits both.

- **Substitution:** when one equation gives $y$ (or $x$) on its own, put it into the other.
- **Elimination:** scale one or both equations so one variable has opposite coefficients, then add.
- Always put the pair back into **both** equations before choosing A–D.

**Work on paper, then pick the pair.**

---

## Easy

### 1. Add the equations

Solve for $x$ and $y$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x + y &= 7 \\
x - y &= 1
\end{aligned}
$$

- **A.** $(3,\ 4)$
- **B.** $(4,\ -3)$
- **C.** $(4,\ 3)$
- **D.** $(5,\ 2)$

### 2. y is already given

Solve for $x$ and $y$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
y &= 2x \\
x + y &= 9
\end{aligned}
$$

- **A.** $(3,\ 6)$
- **B.** $(6,\ 3)$
- **C.** $(3,\ -6)$
- **D.** $(3,\ 5)$

### 3. Substitute y

Solve for $x$ and $y$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x + y &= 10 \\
y &= x + 4
\end{aligned}
$$

- **A.** $(7,\ 3)$
- **B.** $(3,\ -7)$
- **C.** $(4,\ 6)$
- **D.** $(3,\ 7)$

### 4. Add to cancel x

Solve for $x$ and $y$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x + y &= 11 \\
y - x &= 2
\end{aligned}
$$

- **A.** $(5,\ 3)$
- **B.** $(3,\ 5)$
- **C.** $(3,\ -5)$
- **D.** $(3,\ 4)$

## Medium

### 5. Scale one equation

Solve for $x$ and $y$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x + 3y &= 12 \\
x - y &= 1
\end{aligned}
$$

- **A.** $(2,\ 3)$
- **B.** $(3,\ 2)$
- **C.** $(3,\ -2)$
- **D.** $(3,\ 3)$

### 6. Scale and subtract

Solve for $x$ and $y$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x + 2y &= 16 \\
x + y &= 6
\end{aligned}
$$

- **A.** $(2,\ 4)$
- **B.** $(4,\ -2)$
- **C.** $(4,\ 3)$
- **D.** $(4,\ 2)$

### 7. Opposite y coefficients

Solve for $x$ and $y$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
5x - 2y &= 4 \\
x + 2y &= 8
\end{aligned}
$$

- **A.** $(2,\ 3)$
- **B.** $(3,\ 2)$
- **C.** $(2,\ -3)$
- **D.** $(2,\ 2)$

### 8. Substitute from the simpler one

Solve for $x$ and $y$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x + 4y &= 25 \\
2x - y &= 2
\end{aligned}
$$

- **A.** $(4,\ 3)$
- **B.** $(3,\ -4)$
- **C.** $(3,\ 4)$
- **D.** $(3,\ 3)$

## Hard

### 9. Negative solution

Solve for $x$ and $y$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x + 2y &= -1 \\
5x - 4y &= 13
\end{aligned}
$$

- **A.** $(-2,\ 1)$
- **B.** $(1,\ 2)$
- **C.** $(-1,\ 2)$
- **D.** $(1,\ -2)$

### 10. Scale both equations

Solve for $x$ and $y$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x + 5y &= -4 \\
3x - 2y &= 13
\end{aligned}
$$

- **A.** $(3,\ -2)$
- **B.** $(-2,\ 3)$
- **C.** $(3,\ 2)$
- **D.** $(2,\ -3)$

### 11. Fractions first

Solve for $x$ and $y$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{x}{2} + \frac{y}{3} &= 4 \\
x - y &= 3
\end{aligned}
$$

- **A.** $(3,\ 6)$
- **B.** $(6,\ 3)$
- **C.** $(6,\ -3)$
- **D.** $(6,\ 2)$

### 12. A fraction answer

Solve for $x$ and $y$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x + 4y &= 2 \\
5x - 2y &= 25
\end{aligned}
$$

- **A.** $(-\tfrac{5}{2},\ 4)$
- **B.** $(4,\ \tfrac{5}{2})$
- **C.** $(4,\ -\tfrac{5}{2})$
- **D.** $(4,\ -2)$

**1. C — Elimination or substitution, then check both equations**

Add the two equations: the $y$ terms cancel.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x + y) + (x - y) &= 7 + 1 \\
2x &= 8 \\
x &= 4
\end{aligned}
$$

Put $x = 4$ into the first equation.

$$
\begin{aligned}
4 + y &= 7 \\
y &= 3
\end{aligned}
$$

Check in the second: $4 - 3 = 1$. ✓

$$
\boxed{(4,\ 3)}
$$

**2. A — Elimination or substitution, then check both equations**

\begin{aligned}
x + 2x &= 9 \\

x &= 3
\end{aligned}
$$

Then $y = 2x = 6$.

Check: $3 + 6 = 9$. ✓

\boxed{(3,\ 6)}
