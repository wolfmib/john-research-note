# Malta Year 8 Math Chapter 1 — Directed Numbers Practice 3: Multiplying and Dividing

**Quick recap:**

- Multiply or divide the numbers, then work out the sign.
- **Same** signs give $+$.
- **Different** signs give $-$.
- Work from left to right.

**Levels:**

- Questions 1 to 6: Easy
- Questions 7 to 12: Medium
- Questions 13 to 18: Hard

- **A.** $-6  \times  3 < -30  \div  (-2)$

<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. $-12$**

$$
-3  \times  4 = \boxed{-12}
$$

$12$ drops the sign: different signs give $-$.

**2. $-10$**

$$
5  \times  (-2) = \boxed{-10}
$$

$10$ drops the sign.

**3. $18$**

$$
-6  \times  (-3) = \boxed{18}
$$

$-18$: same signs give $+$.

**4. $-4$**

$$
-12  \div  3 = \boxed{-4}
$$

$4$ drops the sign.

**5. $-5$**

$$
20  \div  (-4) = \boxed{-5}
$$

$5$ drops the sign.

**6. $3$**

$$
-18  \div  (-6) = \boxed{3}
$$

$-3$: same signs give $+$.

**7. $30$**

$$
\begin{aligned} -5  \times  (-2)  \times  3 &= 10 \times 3 \\ &= \boxed{30} \end{aligned}
$$

$-30$ forgets that $-5 \times (-2)$ is already positive.

**8. $40$**

$$
\begin{aligned} -4  \times  2  \times  (-5) &= -8 \times (-5) \\ &= \boxed{40} \end{aligned}
$$

$-40$: the two minus signs make a plus.

**9. $-7$**

$$
-56  \div  8 = \boxed{-7}
$$

$7$ drops the sign.

**10. $6$**

$$
-42  \div  (-7) = \boxed{6}
$$

$-6$: same signs give $+$.

**11. $9$**

$$
\begin{aligned} (-3)^{2} &= (-3) \times (-3) \\ &= \boxed{9} \end{aligned}
$$

- $-9$ squares only the 3 — the bracket means $(-3) \times (-3)$.
- $-6$ does $-3 \times 2$.

**12. $-8$**

$$
\begin{aligned} (-2)^{3} &= (-2) \times (-2) \times (-2) \\ &= 4 \times (-2) \\ &= \boxed{-8} \end{aligned}
$$

- $8$: three minus signs multiply to a minus.
- $-6$ does $-2 \times 3$.

**13. $-8$** — The answer 6 is positive, so the box has the same sign as $-48$: negative.

$$
\begin{aligned} \square &= -48 \div 6 \\ &= \boxed{-8} \end{aligned}
$$

- Check: $-48 \div (-8) = 6$.
- $8$ gives $-6$.

**14. $-9$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \square &= 63 \div (-7) \\ &= \boxed{-9} \end{aligned}
$$

- Check: $(-9) \times (-7) = 63$.
- $9$ gives $-63$.

**15. $-30$**

$$
\begin{aligned} (-2)  \times  (-3)  \times  (-5) &= 6 \times (-5) \\ &= \boxed{-30} \end{aligned}
$$

$30$ forgets the third minus sign.

**16. $-4$**

$$
\begin{aligned} -60  \div  (-5)  \div  (-3) &= 12 \div (-3) \\ &= \boxed{-4} \end{aligned}
$$

$4$ forgets the last sign: $12 \div (-3)$ is negative.

**17. $-2$**

$$
\begin{aligned} -3  \times  (-4)  \div  (-6) &= 12 \div (-6) \\ &= \boxed{-2} \end{aligned}
$$

$2$ forgets the last division by a negative.

**18. A — Work out each side**

$$
-6  \times  3 = -18
$$

$$
-30  \div  (-2) = 15
$$

A negative number is smaller than a positive number.

$$
\boxed{-6  \times  3 < -30  \div  (-2)}
$$

B compares 18 and 15 and forgets the signs.

- In $\times$ and $\div$, same signs give $+$.
- Different signs give $-$.
