# SAT Math 7 — Nonlinear Functions Quiz

Five questions on nonlinear functions. **Show your working before you choose or type an answer.**

---

## 1. Tripling every 4 hours

A bacteria culture starts with 500 cells and triples every 4 hours.

How many cells are there after 12 hours?

- **A.** $4{,}500$
- **B.** $6{,}000$
- **C.** $13{,}500$
- **D.** $18{,}000$

## 2. Vertex and direction

Which function has its vertex at $(-2, 7)$ and opens downward?

- **A.** $f(x) = -(x + 2)^2 + 7$
- **B.** $f(x) = -(x - 2)^2 + 7$
- **C.** $f(x) = (x + 2)^2 + 7$
- **D.** $f(x) = -(x + 7)^2 - 2$

## 3. Find the constant first

$$
g(x) = 3^x + k
$$

$k$ is a constant and $g(2) = 5$. What is $g(4)$?

Answer: ______

## 4. Read the growth factor

The population of a town $t$ years after 2020 is modelled by

$$
P(t) = 1200(1.04)^t
$$

Which statement is true?

- **A.** The population increases by 4 people each year.
- **B.** The population was 1,248 in 2020.
- **C.** The population increases by 104% each year.
- **D.** The population increases by 4% each year.

## 5. Shift the zeros

The graph of $y = f(x)$ crosses the $x$-axis at $x = -1$ and $x = 5$.

Where does the graph of $y = f(x - 3)$ cross the $x$-axis?

- **A.** $x = -4$ and $x = 2$
- **B.** $x = 2$ and $x = 8$
- **C.** $x = -3$ and $x = 15$
- **D.** $x = -1$ and $x = 5$

---

<details>
<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. C — 12 hours is 3 tripling periods**

$$
\begin{aligned} N(12) &= 500 \cdot 3^{12/4} \\ &= 500 \cdot 3^3 \\ &= \boxed{13{,}500} \end{aligned}
$$

4,500 uses $3^2$; 18,000 multiplies by $3 \times 12$.

**2. A — a(x − h)² + k with h = −2**

$x - h = x - (-2)$, which is $x + 2$; $k = 7$; $a < 0$ opens down.

$$
\boxed{f(x) = -(x + 2)^2 + 7}
$$

**3. $77$ — Use g(2) to find k**

$$
\begin{aligned} 3^2 + k &= 5 \\ k &= -4 \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned} g(4) &= 3^4 - 4 \\ &= \boxed{77} \end{aligned}
$$

**4. D — b = 1.04 means 4% growth**

$1.04 = 1 + 0.04$: each year is 104% of the year before, an increase of **4%**. In 2020, $t = 0$ and $P = 1200$.

**5. B — f(x − 3) moves the graph right 3**

$f(x - 3) = 0$ when $x - 3 = -1$ or $x - 3 = 5$:

$$
\boxed{x = 2 \text{ and } x = 8}
$$

$-4$ and 2 shift left instead of right.

In a·bᵗ the base is the growth factor (1 ± r); a quadratic's vertex form shows its turning point and f(x − h) moves the graph right h.
