# SAT Math 10 — Geometry and Trigonometry

**SAT domain:** Geometry and Trigonometry (5–7 of the 44 math questions). The reference sheet gives the area and volume formulas.

**Key ideas**
- Angles:
  - a triangle's angles add to $180^\circ$
  - parallel lines: corresponding and alternate angles are equal; co-interior angles add to $180^\circ$
- Similar figures with scale factor $k$: lengths $\times k$, areas $\times k^2$, volumes $\times k^3$.
- Right triangles:
  - $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$
  - $\sin = \dfrac{\text{opp}}{\text{hyp}}$, $\cos = \dfrac{\text{adj}}{\text{hyp}}$, $\tan = \dfrac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}}$
  - $\sin x^\circ = \cos(90^\circ - x^\circ)$
- Special right triangles:
  - $45^\circ$–$45^\circ$–$90^\circ$: sides $1 : 1 : \sqrt{2}$
  - $30^\circ$–$60^\circ$–$90^\circ$: sides $1 : \sqrt{3} : 2$
- Circles:
  - equation $(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$, centre $(h, k)$
  - arc length $= r\theta$, with $\theta$ in radians
  - $\pi$ radians $= 180^\circ$
  - sector area $= \dfrac{\theta}{2\pi} \cdot \pi r^2$

**Watch out**
- Complete the square in **both** $x$ and $y$ to read a circle's radius.

## 1. Exam 1 — Radius from a circle equation

A circle in the $xy$-plane has the equation

$$
x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 8y = 11
$$

What is the radius of the circle?

**A.** $6$

**B.** $\sqrt{11}$

**C.** $36$

**D.** $5$

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

Complete the square in $x$ and in $y$:

$$
(x^2 - 6x + 9) + (y^2 + 8y + 16) = 11 + 9 + 16
$$

$$
(x - 3)^2 + (y + 4)^2 = 36
$$

$r^2 = 36$:

$$
\boxed{r = 6}
$$

**Answer: A** — 36 is $r^2$; $\sqrt{11}$ forgets the added 9 and 16.

</details>

## 2. Exam 2 — Trig ratios in a right triangle

In right triangle $ABC$, angle $C$ is $90^\circ$, $AB = 26$ and

$$
\sin A = \frac{12}{13}
$$

What is $\tan B$? (Type the answer.)

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

$\sin A = \dfrac{BC}{AB}$, the side opposite $A$ over the hypotenuse:

$$
\begin{aligned} BC &= \tfrac{12}{13} \cdot 26 \\ &= 24 \end{aligned}
$$

Pythagoras for the third side:

$$
\begin{aligned} AC^2 &= 26^2 - 24^2 \\ &= 100 \\ AC &= 10 \end{aligned}
$$

For angle $B$, the opposite side is $AC$ and the adjacent side is $BC$:

$$
\begin{aligned} \tan B &= \frac{10}{24} \\ &= \frac{5}{12} \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boxed{\tfrac{5}{12}}
$$

</details>
