# SAT Math 2 — Linear Functions and Graphs Quiz

Five questions on linear functions and graphs. **Show your working before you choose or type an answer.**

---

## 1. Two values of a linear function

For the linear function $f$:

$$
f(2) = 7
$$

$$
f(6) = 19
$$

What is $f(10)$?

- **A.** $27$
- **B.** $30$
- **C.** $37$
- **D.** $31$

## 2. Meaning of the slope

The cost $C$, in euros, of renting a kayak for $h$ hours is

$$
C = 45 + 2.5h
$$

What does 2.5 represent?

- **A.** The fixed fee
- **B.** The cost for each additional hour
- **C.** The total cost for one hour
- **D.** The number of hours that cost €45

## 3. x-intercept from two points

A line passes through $(-3, 5)$ and $(6, -1)$.

What is the $x$-coordinate of its $x$-intercept?

Answer: ______

## 4. Parallel in standard form

Which line is parallel to $2x + 5y = 10$?

- **A.** $y = \tfrac{2}{5}x + 1$
- **B.** $y = -\tfrac{5}{2}x$
- **C.** $4x + 10y = 3$
- **D.** $5x + 2y = 10$

## 5. Equation from a table

$$
\begin{array}{c|ccc} x & 0 & 4 & 8 \\ \hline y & 9 & 15 & 21 \end{array}
$$

Which equation fits the table?

- **A.** $y = \tfrac{3}{2}x + 9$
- **B.** $y = 6x + 9$
- **C.** $y = \tfrac{2}{3}x + 9$
- **D.** $y = \tfrac{3}{2}x + 15$

---

<details>
<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. D — Slope 3, then step 4 more units**

$$
\begin{aligned} m &= \frac{19 - 7}{6 - 2} \\ &= 3 \end{aligned}
$$

From $x = 6$ to $x = 10$ is 4 more units:

$$
\begin{aligned} f(10) &= 19 + 4 \cdot 3 \\ &= \boxed{31} \end{aligned}
$$

**2. B — The slope is the change per hour**

Each extra hour adds €2.5 to $C$. The fixed fee is 45; one hour costs $45 + 2.5 = 47.5$.

**3. $4.5$ — Find the line, then set y = 0**

$$
\begin{aligned} m &= \frac{-1 - 5}{6 - (-3)} \\ &= -\frac{2}{3} \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned} y - 5 &= -\tfrac{2}{3}(x + 3) \\ y &= -\tfrac{2}{3}x + 3 \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned} 0 &= -\tfrac{2}{3}x + 3 \\ x &= \boxed{4.5} \end{aligned}
$$

**4. C — Same slope, −A/B**

$$
\begin{aligned} 2x + 5y = 10 &: \ m = -\tfrac{2}{5} \\ 4x + 10y = 3 &: \ m = -\tfrac{4}{10} \end{aligned}
$$

$-\tfrac{4}{10} = -\tfrac{2}{5}$: the same slope, a different line. $-\tfrac{5}{2}$ is the perpendicular slope.

**5. A — Change in y over change in x**

$$
\begin{aligned} m &= \frac{15 - 9}{4 - 0} \\ &= \frac{3}{2} \end{aligned}
$$

At $x = 0$, $y = 9$, so $b = 9$. Choice $6x$ uses the change in $y$ only; $\tfrac{2}{3}$ is the slope upside down.

Slope is the change per unit and the intercept is the start; parallel lines share a slope and perpendicular slopes multiply to −1.

</details>
