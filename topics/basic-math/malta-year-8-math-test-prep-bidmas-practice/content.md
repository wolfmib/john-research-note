# Malta Year 8 Math — BIDMAS Practice

**12 questions: 4 Easy, 4 Middle, 4 Difficult.**

- Work inside brackets first, then calculate powers.
- Multiplication/division share priority: work left to right.
- Addition/subtraction share priority: work left to right.
- Show your working on paper before entering an answer.

---

## Easy

### 1. Multiplication before addition

Calculate using BIDMAS.

$$
8+3\times4
$$

Answer: ______

### 2. Division before subtraction

Calculate using BIDMAS.

$$
30-18\div3
$$

Answer: ______

### 3. Brackets first

Calculate using BIDMAS.

$$
(7+5)\times2
$$

Answer: ______

### 4. Division and multiplication left to right

Calculate using BIDMAS.

$$
24\div6\times2
$$

Answer: ______

## Middle

### 5. Power, division, addition

Calculate using BIDMAS.

$$
5^2+18\div3
$$

Answer: ______

### 6. A bracket and a power

Calculate using BIDMAS.

$$
48\div(2+4)+3^2
$$

Answer: ______

### 7. Subtract a cube

Calculate using BIDMAS.

$$
7\times6-2^3+4
$$

Answer: ______

### 8. Equal priority with a final subtraction

Calculate using BIDMAS.

$$
36\div3\times2-5
$$

Answer: ______

## Difficult

### 9. Nested divisor and powers

Calculate using BIDMAS.

$$
120\div[3\times(2+3)]+4^2-2^3
$$

Answer: ______

### 10. A squared bracket

Calculate using BIDMAS.

$$
(18-6)^2\div[3\times(2+2)]+5
$$

Answer: ______

### 11. Nested brackets, cube and square

Calculate using BIDMAS.

$$
84\div[2\times(3+4)]+(5-2)^3-4^2
$$

Answer: ______

### 12. A negative final result

Calculate using BIDMAS.

$$
200\div[5\times(3+1)]-(4^2-3^2)\times2
$$

Answer: ______

<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. $20$**

$$
8+3\times4
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&=8+12 \\
&=\boxed{20}
\end{aligned}
$$

Multiply before adding.

**2. $24$**

$$
30-18\div3
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&=30-6 \\
&=\boxed{24}
\end{aligned}
$$

Divide before subtracting.

**3. $24$**

$$
(7+5)\times2
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&=12\times2 \\
&=\boxed{24}
\end{aligned}
$$

Complete the brackets before multiplying.

**4. $8$**

$$
24\div6\times2
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&=4\times2 \\
&=\boxed{8}
\end{aligned}
$$

- Division and multiplication share priority: work left to right.
- Do not multiply 6 by 2 first.

**5. $31$**

$$
5^2+18\div3
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&=25+18\div3 \\
&=25+6 \\
&=\boxed{31}
\end{aligned}
$$

Calculate the power first, then divide, then add.

**6. $17$**

$$
48\div(2+4)+3^2
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&=48\div6+3^2 \\
&=48\div6+9 \\
&=8+9 \\
&=\boxed{17}
\end{aligned}
$$

The entire bracket is the divisor; simplify it before dividing.

**7. $38$**

$$
7\times6-2^3+4
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&=7\times6-8+4 \\
&=42-8+4 \\
&=34+4 \\
&=\boxed{38}
\end{aligned}
$$

Cube first, multiply next, then subtract and add from left to right.

**8. $19$**

$$
36\div3\times2-5
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&=12\times2-5 \\
&=24-5 \\
&=\boxed{19}
\end{aligned}
$$

Divide then multiply from left to right, before the subtraction.

**9. $16$**

$$
120\div[3\times(2+3)]+4^2-2^3
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&=120\div[3\times5]+4^2-2^3 \\
&=120\div15+4^2-2^3 \\
&=120\div15+16-8 \\
&=8+16-8 \\
&=24-8 \\
&=\boxed{16}
\end{aligned}
$$

- Finish the whole divisor bracket before dividing.
- Powers come before the remaining division.

**10. $17$**

$$
(18-6)^2\div[3\times(2+2)]+5
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&=12^2\div[3\times4]+5 \\
&=12^2\div12+5 \\
&=144\div12+5 \\
&=12+5 \\
&=\boxed{17}
\end{aligned}
$$

Square the completed bracket, not only one of the original terms.

**11. $17$**

$$
84\div[2\times(3+4)]+(5-2)^3-4^2
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&=84\div[2\times7]+3^3-4^2 \\
&=84\div14+3^3-4^2 \\
&=84\div14+27-16 \\
&=6+27-16 \\
&=33-16 \\
&=\boxed{17}
\end{aligned}
$$

Work from the innermost brackets outwards, then powers, then division, then addition/subtraction.

**12. $-4$**

$$
200\div[5\times(3+1)]-(4^2-3^2)\times2
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&=200\div[5\times4]-(4^2-3^2)\times2 \\
&=200\div20-(16-9)\times2 \\
&=200\div20-7\times2 \\
&=10-14 \\
&=\boxed{-4}
\end{aligned}
$$

- Finish the subtraction inside the bracket, then multiply its value by 2.
- A correct final answer can be negative.

Brackets, powers, multiplication/division left to right, then addition/subtraction left to right.
