# Malta Year 8 Math — Squares, Cubes & Roots Practice

**6 questions:** 3 on squares and square roots, then 3 on cubes and cube roots.

Work without a calculator and show your calculations on paper.

---

## Squares and square roots

### 1. Square a number

Calculate without a calculator.

$$
14^2
$$

Answer: ______

### 2. Find a square root

Calculate without a calculator.

$$
\sqrt{324}
$$

Answer: ______

### 3. A square plus a root

Calculate without a calculator.

$$
9^2+\sqrt{121}
$$

Answer: ______

## Cubes and cube roots

### 4. Cube a number

Calculate without a calculator.

$$
5^3
$$

Answer: ______

### 5. Find a cube root

Calculate without a calculator.

$$
\sqrt[3]{729}
$$

Answer: ______

### 6. Unit cubes along an edge

A large cube is built from **1000 identical unit cubes**, with no gaps.

How many unit cubes are along **each edge**?

Answer: ______

---

<details>
<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. $196$**

$$
\begin{aligned}
14^2&=14\times14 \\
&=\boxed{196}
\end{aligned}
$$

A square is two copies multiplied together; it is not multiplication by 2.

**2. $18$**

$$
18\times18=324
$$

$$
\sqrt{324}=\boxed{18}
$$

The square-root symbol asks for the non-negative root.

**3. $92$**

$$
\begin{aligned}
9^2+\sqrt{121}&=81+11 \\
&=\boxed{92}
\end{aligned}
$$

Calculate the square and root before adding.

**4. $125$**

$$
\begin{aligned}
5^3&=5\times5\times5 \\
&=\boxed{125}
\end{aligned}
$$

Cubing uses three copies of the number; it is not multiplication by 3.

**5. $9$**

$$
9\times9\times9=729
$$

$$
\sqrt[3]{729}=\boxed{9}
$$

A cube root reverses cubing; a square root is a different operation.

**6. $10$** — The edge count is multiplied three times to give the total.

$$
10\times10\times10=1000
$$

$$
\sqrt[3]{1000}=\boxed{10}
$$

- There are **10 unit cubes along each edge**.
- Dividing the total by 3 would not recover an edge length.

- Square roots undo squares; cube roots undo cubes.
- Work out each power or root before adding.

</details>
