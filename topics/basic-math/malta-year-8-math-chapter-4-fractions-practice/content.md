# Malta Year 8 Math Chapter 4 — Fractions Practice

**Three levels:**

- Easy: two fractions, same or different bottoms
- Medium: three terms with +, − and ×
- Hard: word problems and four terms

**Answers:** a fraction goes in two boxes (top, then bottom), always in its simplest form.

<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. $\frac{7}{9}$**

$$
\frac{2}{9} + \frac{5}{9} = \boxed{\frac{7}{9}}
$$

$\dfrac{7}{18}$ adds the bottoms too; the bottom stays 9.

**2. $\frac{2}{5}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{7}{10} - \frac{3}{10} &= \frac{4}{10} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{2}{5}} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{4}{10}$ is right, but not simplified: divide both by 2.

**3. $\frac{7}{12}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4} &= \frac{4}{12} + \frac{3}{12} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{7}{12}} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{2}{7}$ adds tops and bottoms; first make the bottoms the same.

**4. $\frac{7}{10}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{5} &= \frac{5}{10} + \frac{2}{10} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{7}{10}} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{2}{7}$ adds the bottoms; both bottoms go into 10.

**5. $\frac{1}{2}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{5}{6} - \frac{1}{3} &= \frac{5}{6} - \frac{2}{6} \\ &= \frac{3}{6} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{1}{2}} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{4}{3}$ subtracts the bottoms too; and $\dfrac{3}{6}$ simplifies to $\dfrac{1}{2}$.

**6. $\frac{1}{12}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{3}{4} - \frac{2}{3} &= \frac{9}{12} - \frac{8}{12} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{1}{12}} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{1}{1}$ subtracts tops and bottoms; both bottoms go into 12.

**7. $\frac{1}{2}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{2}{5} + \frac{1}{10} &= \frac{4}{10} + \frac{1}{10} \\ &= \frac{5}{10} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{1}{2}} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{3}{15}$ adds the bottoms; 5 goes into 10, so use tenths.

**8. $\frac{5}{8}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{3}{8} + \frac{1}{4} &= \frac{3}{8} + \frac{2}{8} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{5}{8}} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{4}{12}$ adds the bottoms; 4 goes into 8, so use eighths.

**9. $\frac{3}{10}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{4}{5} - \frac{1}{2} &= \frac{8}{10} - \frac{5}{10} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{3}{10}} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{3}{3}$ subtracts the bottoms; both go into 10.

**10. $\frac{11}{12}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{1}{6} + \frac{3}{4} &= \frac{2}{12} + \frac{9}{12} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{11}{12}} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{4}{10}$ adds the bottoms; 6 and 4 both go into 12.

**11. $\frac{5}{12}$**

$$
\frac{6}{12} + \frac{8}{12} - \frac{9}{12} = \boxed{\frac{5}{12}}
$$

- All three bottoms go into 12.
- Change every fraction before adding.

**12. $\frac{3}{4}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{4}{12} + \frac{3}{12} + \frac{2}{12} &= \frac{9}{12} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{3}{4}} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{9}{12}$ is right, but simplify: divide both by 3.

**13. $\frac{1}{4}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{10}{12} - \frac{3}{12} - \frac{4}{12} &= \frac{3}{12} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{1}{4}} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{3}{12}$ simplifies to $\dfrac{1}{4}$.

**14. $\frac{1}{2}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{2}{3}\times\frac{9}{10}\times\frac{5}{6} &= \frac{1}{1}\times\frac{1}{1}\times\frac{1}{2} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{1}{2}} \end{aligned}
$$

- Trick 1: cancel 2 with 10 (1 and 5), 5 with 5, 9 with 3 (3 and 1), then 3 with 6.
- Multiplying first gives $\dfrac{90}{180}$, the same value but big numbers.

**15. $\frac{1}{2}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{4} &= \frac{2}{4} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{1}{2}} \end{aligned}
$$

- $\times$ comes first: $\dfrac{2}{3}\times\dfrac{3}{8} = \dfrac{1}{4}$.
- Adding first gives a wrong answer.

**16. $\frac{7}{12}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{6} &= \frac{9}{12} - \frac{2}{12} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{7}{12}} \end{aligned}
$$

- $\times$ first: $\dfrac{1}{2}\times\dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{1}{6}$.
- Subtracting first gives $\dfrac{1}{12}$.

**17. $\frac{3}{5}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{4}{10} + \frac{5}{10} - \frac{3}{10} &= \frac{6}{10} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{3}{5}} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{6}{10}$ simplifies to $\dfrac{3}{5}$.

**18. $\frac{9}{8}$**

$$
\frac{7}{8} - \frac{2}{8} + \frac{4}{8} = \boxed{\frac{9}{8}}
$$

- An answer bigger than 1 is fine: $\dfrac{9}{8} = 1\dfrac{1}{8}$.
- Type 9 on top and 8 below.

**19. $\frac{1}{7}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{5}{6}\times\frac{3}{10}\times\frac{4}{7} &= \frac{1}{2}\times\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{4}{7} \\ &= \frac{4}{28} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{1}{7}} \end{aligned}
$$

Trick 1: 5 with 10 and 3 with 6 give $\dfrac{1}{2}\times\dfrac{1}{2}$; then $\dfrac{4}{28} = \dfrac{1}{7}$.

**20. $\frac{17}{12}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{3}{2} + \frac{2}{3} - \frac{3}{4} &= \frac{18}{12} + \frac{8}{12} - \frac{9}{12} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{17}{12}} \end{aligned}
$$

- Change $1\dfrac{1}{2}$ to $\dfrac{3}{2}$ first.
- $\dfrac{17}{12} = 1\dfrac{5}{12}$: type 17 on top and 12 below.

**21. $14$** — Msida: $36\div12 = 3$, $3\times5 = 15$ get off.

$$
36 - 15 = 21
$$

Sliema: $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of 21 is 7.

$$
21 - 7 = \boxed{14}
$$

$9$ takes $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of 36 instead of $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the rest (21).

**22. $\frac{7}{20}$** — The whole is 1:

$$
\begin{aligned} 1 - \frac{1}{4} - \frac{2}{5} &= \frac{20}{20} - \frac{5}{20} - \frac{8}{20} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{7}{20}} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{13}{20}$ is the part she **spent**, not the part left.

**23. $\frac{5}{12}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 1 - \frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{4} &= \frac{12}{12} - \frac{4}{12} - \frac{3}{12} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{5}{12}} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{7}{12}$ is the part eaten.

**24. $15$** — - Walk: $32\div8 = 4$, $4\times3 = 12$.
- Rest: $32 - 12 = 20$.

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{3}{4}\text{ of }20 &= 20\div4\times3 \\ &= \boxed{15} \end{aligned}
$$

$24$ takes $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of all 32 instead of the rest.

**25. $\frac{1}{8}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{3}{4}\div6 &= \frac{3}{4}\times\frac{1}{6} \\ &= \frac{3}{24} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{1}{8}} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{18}{4}$ multiplies by 6 instead of dividing.

**26. $\frac{7}{6}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{2}{3} + \frac{2}{3} - \frac{1}{6} &= \frac{4}{6} + \frac{4}{6} - \frac{1}{6} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{7}{6}} \end{aligned}
$$

$\times$ first with Trick 1: $\dfrac{3}{4}\times\dfrac{8}{9} = \dfrac{1}{1}\times\dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{2}{3}$.

**27. $\frac{3}{4}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{6}{12} + \frac{4}{12} - \frac{3}{12} + \frac{2}{12} &= \frac{9}{12} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{3}{4}} \end{aligned}
$$

All four bottoms go into 12; $\dfrac{9}{12}$ simplifies to $\dfrac{3}{4}$.

**28. $\frac{5}{6}$**

$$
\frac{5}{6} - \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{4} = \boxed{\frac{5}{6}}
$$

- $\times$ first: $\dfrac{2}{3}\times\dfrac{3}{8} = \dfrac{1}{4}$.
- Then $-\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{1}{4}$ is 0.

**29. $\frac{11}{12}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{3}{5}\times\frac{10}{9} + \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{4} &= \frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{4} \\ &= \frac{8}{12} + \frac{6}{12} - \frac{3}{12} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{11}{12}} \end{aligned}
$$

$\div$ first: flip $\dfrac{9}{10}$ to $\dfrac{10}{9}$, then cancel 5 with 10 and 3 with 9.

**30. $\frac{7}{3}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{5}{2} - \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3} &= 2 + \frac{1}{3} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{7}{3}} \end{aligned}
$$

- $\times$ first: $\dfrac{3}{4}\times\dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
- Then $2 + \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{7}{3}$: type 7 on top and 3 below.

- Same bottom first, then add the tops.
- Trick 1: cancel before you multiply.
- $\times$ and $\div$ before $+$ and $-$.
- Always simplify the answer.
