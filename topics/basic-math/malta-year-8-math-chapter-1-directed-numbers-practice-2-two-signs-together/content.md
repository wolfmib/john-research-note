# Malta Year 8 Math Chapter 1 — Directed Numbers Practice 2: Two Signs Together

**Quick recap:**

- Two signs next to each other become one sign.
- $+(+)$ becomes $+$
- $-(-)$ becomes $+$
- $+(-)$ becomes $-$
- $-(+)$ becomes $-$

**Levels:**

- Questions 1 to 6: Easy
- Questions 7 to 12: Medium
- Questions 13 to 18: Hard

## 18. Hard · Compare

Which statement is **true**?

<details>
<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. $3$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 5 + (-2) &= 5 - 2 \\ &= \boxed{3} \end{aligned}
$$

$7$ keeps the plus: $+(-)$ becomes $-$.

**2. $7$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 4 - (-3) &= 4 + 3 \\ &= \boxed{7} \end{aligned}
$$

$1$ ignores the second minus: $-(-)$ becomes $+$.

**3. $5$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 7 - (+2) &= 7 - 2 \\ &= \boxed{5} \end{aligned}
$$

$9$ turns $-(+)$ into $+$, but it stays $-$.

**4. $-4$**

$$
\begin{aligned} -1 + (-3) &= -1 - 3 \\ &= \boxed{-4} \end{aligned}
$$

- $2$ turns $+(-)$ into $+$.
- Adding a negative moves left.

**5. $8$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 2 - (-6) &= 2 + 6 \\ &= \boxed{8} \end{aligned}
$$

$-4$ ignores the second minus.

**6. $5$**

$$
\begin{aligned} -4 + (+9) &= -4 + 9 \\ &= \boxed{5} \end{aligned}
$$

$-13$ turns $+(+)$ into $-$.

**7. $3$**

$$
\begin{aligned} -6 - (-9) &= -6 + 9 \\ &= \boxed{3} \end{aligned}
$$

$-15$ ignores the second minus: $-(-9)$ is $+9$.

**8. $-11$**

$$
\begin{aligned} -3 + (-8) &= -3 - 8 \\ &= \boxed{-11} \end{aligned}
$$

$5$ turns $+(-)$ into $+$.

**9. $-15$**

$$
\begin{aligned} -10 - (+5) &= -10 - 5 \\ &= \boxed{-15} \end{aligned}
$$

$-5$ turns $-(+)$ into $+$.

**10. $-4$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 8 + (-12) &= 8 - 12 \\ &= \boxed{-4} \end{aligned}
$$

$4$ loses the sign: $8 - 12$ goes below zero.

**11. $0$**

$$
\begin{aligned} -7 - (-7) &= -7 + 7 \\ &= \boxed{0} \end{aligned}
$$

$-14$ ignores the second minus.

**12. $11$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 0 - (-11) &= 0 + 11 \\ &= \boxed{11} \end{aligned}
$$

$-11$ ignores the second minus.

**13. $-9$**

$$
\begin{aligned} -4 - (-7) + (-10) - (+2) &= -4 + 7 - 10 - 2 \\ &= 3 - 10 - 2 \\ &= -7 - 2 \\ &= \boxed{-9} \end{aligned}
$$

- $-23$ reads $-(-7)$ as $-7$.
- Change every double sign first, then go left to right.

**14. $-2$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 9 + (-15) - (-4) &= 9 - 15 + 4 \\ &= -6 + 4 \\ &= \boxed{-2} \end{aligned}
$$

$-10$ reads $-(-4)$ as $-4$.

**15. $-16$**

$$
\begin{aligned} -20 - (-8) - (+3) + (-1) &= -20 + 8 - 3 - 1 \\ &= -12 - 3 - 1 \\ &= -15 - 1 \\ &= \boxed{-16} \end{aligned}
$$

$-32$ reads $-(-8)$ as $-8$.

**16. $-8$** — Taking away the box gives a bigger number, so the box is negative.

$$
\begin{aligned} \square &= -5 - 3 \\ &= \boxed{-8} \end{aligned}
$$

- Check: $-5 - (-8) = -5 + 8 = 3$.
- $8$ gives $-5 - 8 = -13$.

**17. $4$** — $+(-6)$ is $-6$, so $\square - 6 = -2$.

$$
\begin{aligned} \square &= -2 + 6 \\ &= \boxed{4} \end{aligned}
$$

- Check: $4 + (-6) = -2$.
- $-8$ takes the 6 away instead of adding it back.

**18. B — Work out each side**

$$
3 - (-4) = 7
$$

$$
-3 + (-4) = -7
$$

Any positive number is bigger than any negative number.

$$
\boxed{3 - (-4) > -3 + (-4)}
$$

C: the digits match, but the signs are opposite.

- Two minus signs side by side make a plus.
- A plus and a minus side by side make a minus.
