<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. $28$**

$$
22 + 6 = \boxed{28}
$$

The step is $+6$; $26$ adds 4.

**2. $21$**

$$
26 - 5 = \boxed{21}
$$

It goes down by 5; $31$ goes up.

**3. $4$**

$$
1 + 3 = \boxed{4}
$$

The step is $+3$ all the way, also past zero.

**4. $48$**

$$
24\times2 = \boxed{48}
$$

The gaps grow (3, 6, 12), so it is $\times2$, not $+$ a fixed number.

**5. $5$**

$$
10\div2 = \boxed{5}
$$

$0$ takes 10 away; the rule is halve.

**6. $7$**

$$
13 - 6 = \boxed{7}
$$

Next term minus this term.

**7. $-4$**

$$
46 - 50 = \boxed{-4}
$$

It goes down, so the difference is negative.

**8. $16$**

$$
\begin{aligned} d &= 30 - 23 \\ &= 7 \\ 9 + 7 &= \boxed{16} \end{aligned}
$$

Check: $16 + 7 = 23$.

**9. $9$**

$$
\begin{aligned} d &= 6 \\ 15 - 6 &= \boxed{9} \end{aligned}
$$

Go back one step: take the difference away.

**10. $44$**

$$
\begin{aligned} d &= 52 - 60 \\ &= -8 \\ 52 - 8 &= \boxed{44} \end{aligned}
$$

Check: $44 - 8 = 36$.

**11. $41$**

$$
4\times10 + 1 = \boxed{41}
$$

$50$ adds before multiplying.

**12. $28$**

$$
5\times6 - 2 = \boxed{28}
$$

$20$ is $5\times(6 - 2)$: times first.

**13. $49$**

$$
2\times20 + 9 = \boxed{49}
$$

$31$ adds 2 instead of doubling.

**14. $3$**

$$
7\times1 - 4 = \boxed{3}
$$

The 1st term uses $n = 1$.

**15. $14$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 30 - 2\times8 &= 30 - 16 \\ &= \boxed{14} \end{aligned}
$$

$224$ is $(30 - 2)\times8$; times first.

**16. $305$**

$$
3\times100 + 5 = \boxed{305}
$$

No need to list 100 terms.

**17. $10$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 3n + 1 &= 31 \\ 3n &= 30 \\ n &= \boxed{10} \end{aligned}
$$

Check: $3\times10 + 1 = 31$.

**18. $10$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 4n - 2 &= 38 \\ 4n &= 40 \\ n &= \boxed{10} \end{aligned}
$$

$-2$ moves across as $+2$ (Trick 2).

**19. $20$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 2n + 5 &= 45 \\ 2n &= 40 \\ n &= \boxed{20} \end{aligned}
$$

$25$ forgets to take the 5 off first.

**20. $10$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 6n + 3 &= 63 \\ 6n &= 60 \\ n &= \boxed{10} \end{aligned}
$$

Check: $6\times10 + 3 = 63$.

**21. B** — - The difference is 4, so start with $4n$.
- At $n = 1$: $4$; the first term is 5, and $5 - 4 = 1$.

$$
\boxed{4n + 1}
$$

$n + 4$ adds 1 each time; $4n + 5$ starts at 9.

**22. C** — - The difference is 3, so start with $3n$.
- At $n = 1$: $3$; the first term is 8, and $8 - 3 = 5$.

$$
\boxed{3n + 5}
$$

$3n + 8$ starts at 11: the 1st term must come out as 8.

**23. C** — - The difference is 6, so start with $6n$.
- At $n = 1$: $6$; the first term is 2, and $2 - 6 = -4$.

$$
\boxed{6n - 4}
$$

$6n + 2$ starts at 8; take 4 away to start at 2.

**24. $64$**

$$
\begin{aligned} d &= 3 \\ 7 - 3 &= 4 \\ & 3n + 4 \\ 3\times20 + 4 &= \boxed{64} \end{aligned}
$$

$67$ uses $3n + 7$: the start needs fixing.

**25. $99$**

$$
\begin{aligned} d &= 2 \\ 1 - 2 &= -1 \\ & 2n - 1 \\ 2\times50 - 1 &= \boxed{99} \end{aligned}
$$

$101$ uses $2n + 1$, which starts at 3.

**26. $19$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 5n + 1 &= 96 \\ 5n &= 95 \\ n &= \boxed{19} \end{aligned}
$$

The rule is $5n + 1$ ($6 - 5 = 1$).

**27. $37$**

$$
\begin{aligned} & 3n + 1 \\ 3\times12 + 1 &= \boxed{37} \end{aligned}
$$

$40$ is $10 + 3\times10$: count from pattern 1, not pattern 3.

**28. $31$**

$$
\begin{aligned} & 2n + 1 \\ 2\times15 + 1 &= \boxed{31} \end{aligned}
$$

Each new triangle adds 2 sticks, so start with $2n$.

**29. $12$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 4n + 2 &= 50 \\ 4n &= 48 \\ n &= \boxed{12} \end{aligned}
$$

The rule is $4n + 2$ ($6 - 4 = 2$).

**30. $39$**

$$
\begin{aligned} & 3n + 9 \\ 3\times10 + 9 &= \boxed{39} \end{aligned}
$$

$42$ uses $3n + 12$; the 1st row must give 12.

- Find the difference first.
- nth term: difference × n, then fix the start (first term − difference).
- Any term: put n into the rule. Which term: set the rule equal to the number and solve.
