**1. $\frac{5}{6}$ — Common denominator 6**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{3} &= \frac{3}{6}+\frac{2}{6} \\
&= \boxed{\frac{5}{6}}
\end{aligned}
$$

**2. $\frac{7}{12}$ — Common denominator 12**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{4} &= \frac{4}{12}+\frac{3}{12} \\
&= \boxed{\frac{7}{12}}
\end{aligned}
$$

**3. $\frac{7}{10}$ — Common denominator 10**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{5} &= \frac{5}{10}+\frac{2}{10} \\
&= \boxed{\frac{7}{10}}
\end{aligned}
$$

**4. $\frac{13}{12}$ — Common denominator 12**

$$
\begin{aligned}
&= \frac{6}{12}+\frac{4}{12}+\frac{3}{12} \\
&= \boxed{\frac{13}{12}}
\end{aligned}
$$

**5. $\frac{47}{60}$ — Common denominator 60**

$$
\begin{aligned}
&= \frac{20}{60}+\frac{15}{60}+\frac{12}{60} \\
&= \boxed{\frac{47}{60}}
\end{aligned}
$$

**6. $6$ — x = 6**

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x+5 &= 17 \\

x &= \boxed{6}
\end{aligned}
$$

**7. $5$ — x = 5**

$$
\begin{aligned}

**8. $8$ — x = 8**

**10. $7$ — x = 7**

$$

**11. $\frac{11}{5}$ — Cross-multiply**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{2x+1}{3} &= \frac{3x-3}{2} \\
2(2x+1) &= 3(3x-3) \\
4x+2 &= 9x-9 \\
11 &= 5x \\
x &= \boxed{\frac{11}{5}}
\end{aligned}
$$

**12. $14$ — Cross-multiply**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{x+4}{2} &= \frac{2x-1}{3} \\
3(x+4) &= 2(2x-1) \\
3x+12 &= 4x-2 \\
14 &= x \\
x &= \boxed{14}
\end{aligned}
$$

**13. $\frac{26}{5}$ — Cross-multiply**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{3x-2}{4} &= \frac{x+5}{3} \\
3(3x-2) &= 4(x+5) \\
9x-6 &= 4x+20 \\
5x &= 26 \\
x &= \boxed{\frac{26}{5}}
\end{aligned}
$$

**14. $\frac{7}{4}$ — Cross-multiply**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{5x+1}{6} &= \frac{2x+3}{4} \\
4(5x+1) &= 6(2x+3) \\
20x+4 &= 12x+18 \\
8x &= 14 \\
x &= \boxed{\frac{7}{4}}
\end{aligned}
$$

**15. $\frac{16}{3}$ — Cross-multiply**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{4x-3}{5} &= \frac{x+2}{2} \\
2(4x-3) &= 5(x+2) \\
8x-6 &= 5x+10 \\
3x &= 16 \\
x &= \boxed{\frac{16}{3}}
\end{aligned}
$$
