# SAT Math 6 — Quadratic and Nonlinear Equations Quiz

Five questions on quadratic and nonlinear equations. **Show your working before you choose or type an answer.**

---

## 1. Sum of the solutions

$$
2x^2 - 7x - 15 = 0
$$

What is the sum of the solutions?

- **A.** $-\tfrac{7}{2}$
- **B.** $\tfrac{7}{2}$
- **C.** $7$
- **D.** $-\tfrac{15}{2}$

## 2. Parabola meets a line

$$
\begin{aligned} y &= -x^2 + 8x - 12 \\ y &= 3 \end{aligned}
$$

The graphs meet at two points. What is the positive difference between their $x$-coordinates?

Answer: ______

## 3. No real solutions

$$
3x^2 + kx + 12 = 0
$$

For which value of $k$ does the equation have **no** real solutions?

- **A.** $-12$
- **B.** $13$
- **C.** $11$
- **D.** $24$

## 4. Rational equation

$$
\frac{x}{x - 2} = \frac{8}{x^2 - 4}
$$

What are all the solutions?

- **A.** $-4$ only
- **B.** $2$ only
- **C.** $-4$ and $2$
- **D.** No solution

## 5. When does the ball land?

A ball is thrown from a cliff. Its height, in metres, after $t$ seconds is

$$
h(t) = -5t^2 + 20t + 25
$$

After how many seconds does the ball hit the ground?

Answer: ______

---

<details>
<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. B — Sum = −b/a, or factor**

$$
\begin{aligned} -\frac{b}{a} &= -\frac{-7}{2} \\ &= \frac{7}{2} \end{aligned}
$$

Factor check: $(2x + 3)(x - 5) = 0$ gives $x = -\tfrac{3}{2}$ and $x = 5$; their sum is $\tfrac{7}{2}$.

**2. $2$ — Set equal and factor**

$$
\begin{aligned} -x^2 + 8x - 12 &= 3 \\ x^2 - 8x + 15 &= 0 \\ (x - 3)(x - 5) &= 0 \end{aligned}
$$

$x = 3$ and $x = 5$: the difference is $\boxed{2}$.

**3. C — Need D < 0**

$$
\begin{aligned} D &= k^2 - 4(3)(12) \\ &= k^2 - 144 \end{aligned}
$$

$D < 0$ needs $-12 < k < 12$. Only 11 works. $k = -12$ gives $D = 0$: exactly one solution.

**4. A — Multiply by (x − 2)(x + 2), then check**

$$
\begin{aligned} x(x + 2) &= 8 \\ x^2 + 2x - 8 &= 0 \\ (x + 4)(x - 2) &= 0 \end{aligned}
$$

$x = 2$ makes the denominators 0, so it is extraneous. Only $\boxed{x = -4}$.

**5. $5$ — Ground means h = 0**

$$
\begin{aligned} -5t^2 + 20t + 25 &= 0 \\ t^2 - 4t - 5 &= 0 \\ (t - 5)(t + 1) &= 0 \end{aligned}
$$

Time cannot be negative, so $\boxed{t = 5}$.

Set the equation to zero, factor or use the formula, read the discriminant for the number of solutions, and check radical and rational answers.

</details>
