# SAT Math 2 — Linear Functions and Graphs

**SAT domain:** Algebra (13–15 of the 44 math questions).

**Key ideas**
- Slope-intercept form:
  - $y = mx + b$
- Slope from two points:
  - $m = \dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$
- In a context, the slope is the change per unit, and $b$ is the starting value (when $x = 0$).
- Standard form $Ax + By = C$:
  - slope $= -\dfrac{A}{B}$
  - $x$-intercept $= \dfrac{C}{A}$
  - $y$-intercept $= \dfrac{C}{B}$
- Parallel lines have equal slopes.
- Perpendicular slopes multiply to $-1$: the new slope is the negative reciprocal.

**Watch out**
- Keep the order the same on top and bottom of the slope formula.
- A decreasing quantity has a negative slope.

## 1. Exam 1 — Build the model

A water tank holds 840 litres. It drains at a constant rate, and after 15 minutes it holds 630 litres.

Which function gives the volume $V$, in litres, after $t$ minutes?

**A.** $V(t) = 840 - 14t$

**B.** $V(t) = 630 - 14t$

**C.** $V(t) = 840 - 15t$

**D.** $V(t) = 840 + 14t$

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

Slope from the two points $(0, 840)$ and $(15, 630)$:

$$
\begin{aligned} m &= \frac{630 - 840}{15 - 0} \\ &= -14 \end{aligned}
$$

The starting volume is the intercept, 840:

$$
\boxed{V(t) = 840 - 14t}
$$

**Answer: A** — 630 is the volume after 15 minutes, not at the start.

</details>

## 2. Exam 2 — Perpendicular line

Line $\ell$ passes through $(2, -1)$ and is perpendicular to the line

$$
3x - 4y = 8
$$

What is the $y$-intercept of line $\ell$? (Type the answer.)

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

Slope of $3x - 4y = 8$:

$$
\begin{aligned} m &= -\frac{3}{-4} \\ &= \frac{3}{4} \end{aligned}
$$

Perpendicular slope, the negative reciprocal:

$$
m_\ell = -\frac{4}{3}
$$

Use the point $(2, -1)$ in $y = m_\ell x + b$:

$$
\begin{aligned} -1 &= -\tfrac{4}{3}(2) + b \\ -1 &= -\tfrac{8}{3} + b \\ b &= \tfrac{5}{3} \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boxed{b = \tfrac{5}{3}}
$$

</details>
