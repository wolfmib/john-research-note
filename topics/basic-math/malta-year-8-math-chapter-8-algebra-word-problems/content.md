# Malta Year 8 Math Chapter 8 — Algebra Word Problems

- Each story has two things with unknown prices.
- Read the story and write two equations yourself, one for each person or family.
- Solve them to find both prices.
- In stories 3 and 4, part (c) asks how many ways there are to spend a budget exactly.
- Type money as a number only, for example 2.5 for 2.50 euros.

**1. $1.5$** — Call the price of one pen $x$ euros and the price of one notebook $y$ euros.

Write one equation for each:

$$
4x + 3y = 15 \;\;\text{(1)}
$$

$$
2x + 5y = 18 \;\;\text{(2)}
$$

Make the number of pens the same: multiply (1) by 2 and (2) by 4.

$$
8x + 6y = 30
$$

$$
8x + 20y = 72
$$

Take one away from the other, so the pens disappear:

$$
14y = 42
$$

$$
\begin{aligned} y &= 42 \div 14 \\ &= 3 \end{aligned}
$$

Put $y = 3$ back into (1):

$$
4x + 3 \times 3 = 15
$$

$$
\begin{aligned} 4x &= 15 - 9 \\ &= 6 \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned} x &= 6 \div 4 \\ &= 1.50 \end{aligned}
$$

Check with (2): 2 × 1.50 + 5 × 3 = 18.

It matches.

$$
\boxed{1.50}\text{ euros}
$$
