# AP Precalculus — Recap Set A (Unit 1 to 2.10)

**13 questions, Unit 1 to 2.10, in topic order.**

- 9 questions ask for a calculation: work it out on paper, then pick A–D.
- 4 questions ask for the idea behind a topic.
- The title of each question names the topic (1.2, 2.5, …).

**A calculator is allowed. Show your working before choosing A–D.**

---

## 1. 1.2 · Average rate of change

$f(x) = x^2 - 3x$. Find the average rate of change of $f$ on $[1,\,4]$.

- **A.** $2$
- **B.** $6$
- **C.** $1$
- **D.** $-2$

## 2. 1.3 · Rate of change from a table

$$
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
x & 0 & 1 & 2 & 3\\
\hline
f(x) & 2 & 5 & 10 & 17
\end{array}
$$

Which statement about $f$ on this interval is true?

- **A.** The rate of change is constant, so $f$ is linear
- **B.** The rate of change is decreasing, so the graph is concave down
- **C.** The rate of change is increasing, so the graph is concave up
- **D.** The rate of change is negative, so $f$ is decreasing

## 3. 1.5 · Zeros of a polynomial

Find all real zeros of

$$
p(x) = x^3 - 4x^2 - 5x
$$

- **A.** $x = 0,\ -5,\ 1$
- **B.** $x = 0,\ 5,\ -1$
- **C.** $x = 5,\ -1$
- **D.** $x = 0,\ 4,\ 5$

## 4. 1.9 · Vertical asymptote or hole

$$
r(x) = \frac{x^2 - 9}{x^2 - x - 6}
$$

Where is the **vertical asymptote** of $r$?

- **A.** $x = 3$
- **B.** $x = -3$
- **C.** $x = 2$
- **D.** $x = -2$

## 5. 1.12 · Transformations

The point $(1,\,4)$ is on the graph of $f$. Let

$$
g(x) = 2\,f(x - 3) + 1
$$

Which point is on the graph of $g$?

- **A.** $(4,\,9)$
- **B.** $(-2,\,9)$
- **C.** $(4,\,7)$
- **D.** $(-2,\,7)$

The $x$ values go up by the same amount. Which type of function fits the data?

## 7. 2.1 · Arithmetic sequence

A geometric sequence starts $5,\ 10,\ 20,\ 40,\ \dots$. Find $a_8$.

## 9. 2.5 · Exponential model in context

A colony of $2000$ bacteria **doubles every 6 hours**. About how many bacteria are there after $15$ hours? (Calculator allowed.)

- **A.** about $11{,}300$
- **B.** about $10{,}000$
- **C.** about $8{,}000$
- **D.** about $16{,}000$

## 10. 2.6 · Residuals

A model predicts $13$ for $x = 4$; the actual data value there is $10$. The residual at $x = 4$ is $-3$.

What does this residual tell you?

- **A.** The model **overestimates** the data at $x = 4$ by $3$
- **B.** The model underestimates the data at $x = 4$ by $3$
- **C.** The model is exact at $x = 4$
- **D.** The model must be replaced by an exponential model

## 11. 2.7 · Composition

$f(x) = 3x - 1$ and $g(x) = x^2$. Find $f(g(2))$.

- **A.** $25$
- **B.** $5$
- **C.** $11$
- **D.** $7$

## 12. 2.9 · Logarithmic expressions

Solve for $x$:

$$
2^{x} = \frac{1}{32}
$$

- **A.** $x = 5$
- **B.** $x = -5$
- **C.** $x = \tfrac{1}{5}$
- **D.** $x = -32$

## 13. 2.10 · The logarithmic function

$f(x) = \log_b x$ with $b > 1$. Which statement is true for **every** such base $b$?

- **A.** $f(0) = 0$
- **B.** The domain of $f$ is all real numbers
- **C.** $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{b^{x}}$
- **D.** $f(1) = 0$

<details>
<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. A — Change in output over change in input**

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(4) &= 16 - 12 = 4 \\
f(1) &= 1 - 3 = -2 \\
\frac{f(4) - f(1)}{4 - 1} &= \frac{4 - (-2)}{3} \\
&= \boxed{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

- Not $6$: that is $f(4) - f(1)$ without dividing by the width $3$.
- Not $-2$: that is $f(1)$, not a rate.

**2. C — Look at the differences**

Differences between consecutive outputs:

$$
\begin{aligned}
5 - 2 &= 3 \\
10 - 5 &= 5 \\
17 - 10 &= 7
\end{aligned}
$$

- The differences grow: the rate of change is **increasing**.
- Increasing rate of change = **concave up**.
- Not linear: a linear function has the same difference every step.
- Not negative: every difference is positive, $f$ is increasing.

**3. B — Take out the common factor, then factor the quadratic**

$$
\begin{aligned}
p(x) &= x\,(x^2 - 4x - 5) \\
&= x\,(x - 5)(x + 1)
\end{aligned}
$$

Each factor gives a zero:

$$
\boxed{x = 0,\quad x = 5,\quad x = -1}
$$

- Not $5, -1$ only: the factor $x$ gives the zero $x = 0$ too.
- Not $-5, 1$: $(x-5)(x+1)$, so the signs are $+5$ and $-1$.

**4. D — Factor first; a common factor is a hole, not an asymptote**

$$
\begin{aligned}
r(x) &= \frac{(x - 3)(x + 3)}{(x - 3)(x + 2)} \\
&= \frac{x + 3}{x + 2}, \quad x \ne 3
\end{aligned}
$$

- $x = 3$ cancels: a **hole** at $x = 3$.
- $x = -2$ stays in the bottom: $\boxed{\text{vertical asymptote } x = -2}$.
- Not $x = -3$: that makes the top zero, so it is a zero of $r$, not an asymptote.

**5. A — Inside moves x, outside moves y**

- $x - 3$ inside: shift **right** by $3$: $x = 1 \to 4$.
- $2(\dots) + 1$ outside: stretch by $2$, then up $1$: $y = 4 \to 2\cdot4 + 1 = 9$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
g(4) &= 2\,f(4 - 3) + 1 \\
&= 2\,f(1) + 1 \\
&= 2\cdot4 + 1 \\
&= \boxed{9}
\end{aligned}
$$

- Not $(-2, 9)$: $x - 3$ shifts right, not left.
- Not $(4, 7)$: the $+1$ comes after the $\times2$.

**6. C — Equal steps in x, constant ratio in y**

$$
\begin{aligned}
12 \div 8 &= 1.5 \\
18 \div 12 &= 1.5 \\
27 \div 18 &= 1.5
\end{aligned}
$$

- Each step **multiplies** $y$ by the same factor $1.5$: $\boxed{\text{exponential}}$.
- Linear would add the same amount each step ($4, 6, 9$ are not equal).
- Quadratic would have equal second differences ($2, 3$ are not equal).

**7. B — a_n = a_1 + (n − 1)d**

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_{15} &= 7 + (15 - 1)\cdot4 \\
&= 7 + 56 \\
&= \boxed{63}
\end{aligned}
$$

- Not $67$: that uses $15\cdot4$; the first term already counts, so only $14$ steps of $4$.

**8. D — a_n = a_1 · r^(n − 1)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
r &= 10 \div 5 = 2 \\
a_8 &= 5 \cdot 2^{\,8-1} \\
&= 5 \cdot 128 \\
&= \boxed{640}
\end{aligned}
$$

- Not $1280$: that is $5\cdot2^8$, one multiplication too many.
- Not $40$: that is $a_4$.

**9. A — Count the doublings as a fraction**

Number of doublings in $15$ hours: $15 \div 6 = 2.5$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
P &= 2000 \cdot 2^{15/6} \\
&= 2000 \cdot 2^{2.5} \\
&\approx 2000 \cdot 5.657 \\
&\approx \boxed{11{,}300}
\end{aligned}
$$

- Not $8{,}000$: that is $2$ doublings (12 hours), stopping early.
- Not $16{,}000$: that is $3$ doublings (18 hours).
- Not $10{,}000$: $2.5$ doublings is **not** $2.5 \times 2000$; the growth is multiplicative.

**10. A — residual = actual − predicted**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{residual} &= \text{actual} - \text{predicted} \\
&= 10 - 13 \\
&= -3
\end{aligned}
$$

- A **negative** residual means the prediction was too high: the model $\boxed{\text{overestimates}}$.
- One residual says nothing about which model type to use; that needs the whole residual plot.

**11. C — Inside first**

$$
\begin{aligned}
g(2) &= 2^2 = 4 \\
f(g(2)) &= f(4) \\
&= 3\cdot4 - 1 \\
&= \boxed{11}
\end{aligned}
$$

- Not $25$: that is $g(f(2)) = (3\cdot2 - 1)^2$, the other order.
- Not $5$: that is $f(2)$, the $g$ was skipped.

**12. B — A fraction below 1 needs a negative exponent**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{32} &= \frac{1}{2^5} \\
&= 2^{-5} \\
x &= \log_2 \tfrac{1}{32} \\
&= \boxed{-5}
\end{aligned}
$$

- Not $5$: $2^5 = 32$, not $\tfrac{1}{32}$.
- Not $\tfrac{1}{5}$: the minus sign goes on the exponent, not under it.

**13. D — b to the power 0 is 1**

$$
\begin{aligned}
b^0 &= 1 \\
\log_b 1 &= \boxed{0}
\end{aligned}
$$

- Not $f(0) = 0$: $\log_b 0$ has no value; the input must be positive.
- Not all reals: the domain is $x > 0$ (the range is all reals).
- Not $\tfrac{1}{b^x}$: the log is the **inverse** of $b^x$, not its reciprocal.

Unit 1 is about how functions change; Unit 2 is about functions that add (linear) or multiply (exponential), and running them backwards (logarithms).

</details>
