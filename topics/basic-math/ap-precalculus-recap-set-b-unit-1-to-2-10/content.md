# AP Precalculus — Recap Set B (Unit 1 to 2.10)

**13 questions, Unit 1 to 2.10, in topic order.**

- 9 questions ask for a calculation: work it out on paper, then pick A–D.
- 4 questions ask for the idea behind a topic.
- The title of each question names the topic (1.2, 2.5, …).

**A calculator is allowed. Show your working before choosing A–D.**

## 1. 1.2 · Average rate of change

$f(x) = 2x^2 + 1$. Find the average rate of change of $f$ on $[0,\,3]$.

- **A.** $18$
- **B.** $6$
- **C.** $9$
- **D.** $3$

## 2. 1.3 · Rate of change from a table

$$
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
x & 0 & 1 & 2 & 3\\
\hline
f(x) & 20 & 17 & 12 & 5
\end{array}
$$

Which statement about $f$ on this interval is true?

- **A.** $f$ is decreasing and the graph is concave down
- **B.** $f$ is decreasing and the graph is concave up
- **C.** $f$ is linear with slope $-3$
- **D.** $f$ is increasing and the graph is concave down

## 3. 1.5 · Zeros and multiplicity

$$
p(x) = x^4 - 9x^2

At which zero does the graph **touch the x-axis without crossing** it?

- **A.** $x = 3$

## 4. 1.10 · Holes

Where does the graph of $r$ have a **hole**?

## 13. 2.10 · The logarithmic function

$f(x) = \log_2 x$. If the input $x$ is **multiplied by 8**, what happens to the output $f(x)$?

- **A.** It increases by $3$
- **B.** It is multiplied by $8$
- **C.** It increases by $8$
- **D.** It is multiplied by $3$

<details>
<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. B — Change in output over change in input**

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(3) &= 2\cdot9 + 1 = 19 \\
f(0) &= 1 \\
\frac{f(3) - f(0)}{3 - 0} &= \frac{18}{3} \\
&= \boxed{6}
\end{aligned}
$$

- Not $18$: that is the change in $f$, not divided by the width $3$.

**2. A — Look at the differences**

$$
\begin{aligned}
17 - 20 &= -3 \\
12 - 17 &= -5 \\
5 - 12 &= -7
\end{aligned}
$$

- All differences negative: $f$ is **decreasing**.
- The differences get more negative: the rate of change is decreasing, so **concave down**.
- Not linear: the differences are not all the same.

**3. C — Even multiplicity touches, odd crosses**

$$
\begin{aligned}
p(x) &= x^2\,(x^2 - 9) \\
&= x^2\,(x - 3)(x + 3)
\end{aligned}
$$

- $x = 0$ has multiplicity $2$ (even): the graph **touches** there. $\boxed{x = 0}$
- $x = 3$ and $x = -3$ have multiplicity $1$: the graph crosses.
- $x = 9$ is not a zero: $p(9) \ne 0$.

**4. D — A factor on top and bottom is a hole**

- $(x - 2)$ is a factor of both the top and the bottom: it cancels, leaving a **hole** at $\boxed{x = 2}$.
- $(x - 5)$ stays in the bottom only: vertical asymptote at $x = 5$.
- $(x + 1)$ stays on top only: zero at $x = -1$.

**5. A — Inside moves x, outside moves y**

- $x + 2$ inside: shift **left** by $2$: $x = 3 \to 1$.
- $-(\dots) - 4$ outside: reflect over the x-axis, then down $4$: $y = 5 \to -5 - 4 = -9$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
g(1) &= -f(1 + 2) - 4 \\
&= -f(3) - 4 \\
&= -5 - 4 \\
&= \boxed{-9}
\end{aligned}
$$

- Not $(5, \dots)$: $x + 2$ shifts left, not right.
- Not $(1, 1)$: the reflection makes $5$ into $-5$ before the $-4$.

**6. B — Equal steps in x, constant difference in y**

$$
\begin{aligned}
10 - 3 &= 7 \\
17 - 10 &= 7 \\
24 - 17 &= 7
\end{aligned}
$$

- Each step **adds** the same $7$: $\boxed{\text{linear}}$, slope $7$.
- Exponential would multiply by the same factor ($10/3 \ne 17/10$).

**7. D — Find d from the gap, then a_1**

$$
\begin{aligned}
d &= \frac{27 - 11}{7 - 3} = \frac{16}{4} = 4 \\
a_1 &= a_3 - 2d = 11 - 8 = 3 \\
a_{20} &= 3 + (20 - 1)\cdot4 \\
&= 3 + 76 \\
&= \boxed{79}
\end{aligned}
$$

- Not $83$: that uses $20$ steps instead of $19$.

**8. C — a_n = a_1 · r^(n − 1)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
a_6 &= 96 \cdot \left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right)^{5} \\
&= \frac{96}{32} \\
&= \boxed{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

- Not $1.5$: that is $a_7$, one halving too many.
- Check by listing: $96, 48, 24, 12, 6, 3$.

**9. A — Losing 15 % means multiplying by 0.85**

$$
\begin{aligned}
V &= 24000 \cdot 0.85^{4} \\
&\approx 24000 \cdot 0.522 \\
&\approx \boxed{€12{,}500}
\end{aligned}
$$

- Not $9{,}600$: that subtracts $15\%$ of the **original** four times ($24000 - 4\cdot3600$); decay is multiplicative.
- Not $14{,}700$: that is $24000 \cdot 0.85^{3}$, only $3$ years.

**10. C — Residuals should look random**

- Positive, then negative, then positive: the data bends around the line, a **curved pattern**.
- A good model leaves residuals scattered randomly above and below zero.
- Summing to zero happens for every least-squares line, so it proves nothing.

$$
\boxed{\text{pattern} \Rightarrow \text{try a different model}}
$$

**11. B — Which input gives the output 3?**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{x - 4}{2} &= 3 \\
x - 4 &= 6 \\
x &= \boxed{10}
\end{aligned}
$$

Check: $f(10) = \frac{10 - 4}{2} = 3$.

- Not $-0.5$: that is $f(3)$, the function itself, not its inverse.
- Not $2$: $f^{-1}$ is not $\frac{1}{f}$.

**12. D — Write both sides as powers of 2**

$$
\begin{aligned}
(2^2)^{x} &= 2^5 \\
2^{2x} &= 2^5 \\
2x &= 5 \\
x &= \boxed{2.5}
\end{aligned}
$$

Check: $4^{2.5} = 4^2 \cdot 4^{0.5} = 16 \cdot 2 = 32$.

- Not $8$: that is $32 \div 4$; a log asks for an exponent.
- Not $3$: $4^3 = 64$, too big.

**13. A — Inputs multiply, outputs add**

- $\times 8$ is three doublings: $8 = 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2$.
- Each doubling of the input adds $1$ to $\log_2 x$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\log_2 (8x) &= \log_2 8 + \log_2 x \\
&= 3 + \log_2 x
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boxed{\text{output} + 3}
$$

- Check with $x = 4$: $\log_2 4 = 2$ and $\log_2 32 = 5$. Up by $3$.
- For an exponential it is the other way round: inputs add, outputs multiply.

Unit 1 is about how functions change; Unit 2 is about functions that add (linear) or multiply (exponential), and running them backwards (logarithms).
