# SAT Math 3 — Systems of Linear Equations Quiz

Five questions on systems of linear equations. **Show your working before you choose or type an answer.**

---

## 1. Ask for x + y

$$
\begin{aligned} 3x + 2y &= 17 \\ x + 2y &= 7 \end{aligned}
$$

What is the value of $x + y$?

- **A.** $5$
- **B.** $6$
- **C.** $24$
- **D.** $7$

## 2. Substitute into fractions

$$
\begin{aligned} y &= 2x - 3 \\ \tfrac{1}{2}x + \tfrac{1}{3}y &= 6 \end{aligned}
$$

The solution is $(x, y)$. What is the value of $y$?

Answer: ______

## 3. Count the solutions

$$
\begin{aligned} 6x - 4y &= 10 \\ 9x - 6y &= 15 \end{aligned}
$$

How many solutions does the system have?

- **A.** Zero
- **B.** Exactly one
- **C.** Exactly two
- **D.** Infinitely many

## 4. Coffee blend

A café mixes beans that cost €12 per kg with beans that cost €20 per kg.

It makes 40 kg of a blend that costs €15 per kg.

How many kilograms of the €12 beans are used?

- **A.** $15$
- **B.** $20$
- **C.** $25$
- **D.** $30$

## 5. Where two lines meet

$$
\begin{aligned} y &= -x + 7 \\ y &= \tfrac{2}{3}x + 2 \end{aligned}
$$

The solution is $(a, b)$. What is the value of $ab$?

- **A.** $12$
- **B.** $7$
- **C.** $3$
- **D.** $4$

---

<details>
<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. B — Subtract, then substitute**

Subtract the second equation from the first:

$$
\begin{aligned} 2x &= 10 \\ x &= 5 \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned} 5 + 2y &= 7 \\ y &= 1 \end{aligned}
$$

$$
x + y = \boxed{6}
$$

5 is $x$ alone. Adding the equations gives $4x + 4y = 24$, so 24 is $4(x + y)$.

**2. $9$ — Substitute, then multiply by 6**

$$
\begin{aligned} \tfrac{1}{2}x + \tfrac{1}{3}(2x - 3) &= 6 \\ 3x + 2(2x - 3) &= 36 \\ 7x - 6 &= 36 \\ x &= 6 \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned} y &= 2(6) - 3 \\ &= \boxed{9} \end{aligned}
$$

**3. D — One equation is a multiple of the other**

Multiply the first equation by $\tfrac{3}{2}$:

$$
9x - 6y = 15
$$

That is the second equation: the same line, so **infinitely many** solutions. Two lines can never meet in exactly two points.

**4. C — Mass and cost equations**

$$
\begin{aligned} a + b &= 40 \\ 12a + 20b &= 15 \cdot 40 \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned} 12a + 20(40 - a) &= 600 \\ -8a + 800 &= 600 \\ a &= \boxed{25} \end{aligned}
$$

15 kg is the €20 beans. Sense check: €15 is nearer €12 than €20, so more of the cheap beans.

**5. A — Set the two right sides equal**

$$
\begin{aligned} -x + 7 &= \tfrac{2}{3}x + 2 \\ 5 &= \tfrac{5}{3}x \\ x &= 3 \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned} y &= -3 + 7 \\ &= 4 \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned} ab &= 3 \cdot 4 \\ &= \boxed{12} \end{aligned}
$$

Substitute or eliminate; compare the coefficient ratios to spot no solution or infinitely many before you solve.

</details>
