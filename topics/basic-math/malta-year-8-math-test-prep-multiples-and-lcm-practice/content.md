# Malta Year 8 Math — LCM & HCF Practice

**8 questions:** 4 with two numbers (find HCF and LCM), then 4 with three numbers (find LCM).

- Every starting number in the two-number questions is below 50.
- In a common-factor ladder, divide only when a prime divides **every** entry.
- The product on the left is the **HCF**.
- For two numbers, multiply the left factors and the bottom entries to get the **LCM**.
- For three numbers, check that no two bottom entries share a factor before multiplying them; otherwise find the LCM of the bottom row first.

**1. B** — - Divide only by a prime that divides **every** number in the row.
- Stop when no prime divides them all.

$$
\begin{array}{r|rr}
2 & 12 & 20 \\ 2 & 6 & 10 \\  & 3 & 5
\end{array}
$$

**HCF: multiply the common factors on the left.**

$$
\operatorname{HCF}=2\times2
$$

$$
\operatorname{HCF}=\boxed{4}
$$

**LCM: left factors × bottom entries.** No two bottom entries share a factor greater than 1.

$$
\operatorname{LCM}=2\times2\times3\times5
$$

$$
\operatorname{LCM}=\boxed{60}
$$

**Check:**

$$
4\times60=240
$$

$$
12\times20=240
$$

- **Avoid the trap:** multiplying the two starting numbers counts their common factors twice.
- The HCF and LCM columns also have different meanings.
