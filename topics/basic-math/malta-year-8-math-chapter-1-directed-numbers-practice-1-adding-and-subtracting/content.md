# Malta Year 8 Math Chapter 1 — Directed Numbers Practice 1: Adding and Subtracting

**Quick recap:**

- Start at the first number on the number line.
- $+$ means move **right**.
- $-$ means move **left**.
- Work from left to right.

**Levels:**

- Questions 1 to 6: Easy
- Questions 7 to 12: Medium
- Questions 13 to 18: Hard

<details>
<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. $2$**

$$
-2 + 4 = \boxed{2}
$$

- $-6$ adds the sizes and keeps the minus.
- Four steps right from $-2$ passes zero and lands on 2.

**2. $-4$**

$$
3 - 7 = \boxed{-4}
$$

$4$ forgets the sign: 7 steps left from 3 goes past zero.

**3. $3$**

$$
-5 + 8 = \boxed{3}
$$

$-3$ has the wrong sign: the positive part (8) is bigger, so the answer is positive.

**4. $-3$**

$$
6 - 9 = \boxed{-3}
$$

$3$ forgets the sign: you take away more than you have.

**5. $-5$**

$$
-1 - 4 = \boxed{-5}
$$

- $3$ treats it as $-1 + 4$.
- Moving left from $-1$ goes further below zero.

**6. $0$**

$$
-7 + 7 = \boxed{0}
$$

- $-14$ adds the sizes.
- A number plus its opposite is always 0.

**7. $-10$**

$$
\begin{aligned} -8 + 3 - 5 &= -5 - 5 \\ &= \boxed{-10} \end{aligned}
$$

- $0$ does $3 - 5$ first.
- Work left to right.

**8. $-3$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 4 - 10 + 3 &= -6 + 3 \\ &= \boxed{-3} \end{aligned}
$$

- $-9$ does $10 + 3$ first.
- Work left to right.

**9. $3$**

$$
\begin{aligned} -6 - 2 + 11 &= -8 + 11 \\ &= \boxed{3} \end{aligned}
$$

$-3$ has the wrong sign: $-8 + 11$ is positive.

**10. $6$** — From $-4$ to $2$ is 6 steps right.

$$
\begin{aligned} \square &= 2 - (-4) \\ &= 2 + 4 \\ &= \boxed{6} \end{aligned}
$$

- Check: $-4 + 6 = 2$.
- $-2$ drops the sign of $-4$: $-4 + (-2) = -6$.

**11. $5$** — Add the 6 back:

$$
\begin{aligned} \square &= -1 + 6 \\ &= \boxed{5} \end{aligned}
$$

- Check: $5 - 6 = -1$.
- $-7$ takes the 6 away instead of adding it back.

**12. $8$** — From 3 down to $-5$ is 8 steps left.

$$
\begin{aligned} \square &= 3 - (-5) \\ &= 3 + 5 \\ &= \boxed{8} \end{aligned}
$$

- Check: $3 - 8 = -5$.
- $2$ gives $3 - 2 = 1$.

**13. $1$**

$$
\begin{aligned} -12 + 7 - 9 + 15 &= -5 - 9 + 15 \\ &= -14 + 15 \\ &= \boxed{1} \end{aligned}
$$

Most slips happen in one step — check each line of the working.

**14. $-11$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 20 - 35 + 8 - 4 &= -15 + 8 - 4 \\ &= -7 - 4 \\ &= \boxed{-11} \end{aligned}
$$

$11$ loses the sign at the first step: $20 - 35$ is negative.

**15. $-9$**

$$
\begin{aligned} -25 + 40 - 18 - 6 &= 15 - 18 - 6 \\ &= -3 - 6 \\ &= \boxed{-9} \end{aligned}
$$

$9$ loses the sign: $15 - 18$ is already negative.

**16. $9$** — Join the numbers first: $-15 - 4 = -19$, so $-19 + \square = -10$.

$$
\begin{aligned} \square &= -10 - (-19) \\ &= -10 + 19 \\ &= \boxed{9} \end{aligned}
$$

- Check: $-15 + 9 - 4 = -10$.
- $5$ forgets the $-4$.

**17. $-8$** — Join the numbers first: $-9 + 3 = -6$, so $\square - 6 = -14$.

$$
\begin{aligned} \square &= -14 + 6 \\ &= \boxed{-8} \end{aligned}
$$

- Check: $-8 - 9 + 3 = -14$.
- $-20$ takes the 6 away instead of adding it back.

**18. B — Work out each side**

$$
-8 + 5 = -3
$$

$$
-2 - 4 = -6
$$

$-3$ is to the right of $-6$ on the number line.

$$
\boxed{-8 + 5 > -2 - 4}
$$

A compares 3 and 6 and forgets that both are negative.

- Adding a positive moves right; taking away moves left.
- A number plus its opposite is 0.

</details>
