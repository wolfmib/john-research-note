# SAT Math 9 — Statistics and Probability Quiz

Five questions on statistics and probability. **Show your working before you choose or type an answer.**

---

## 1. A sixth number

The mean of five numbers is 14.

A sixth number is added, and the mean of the six numbers is 15. What is the sixth number?

Answer: ______

## 2. Compare the spread

Set A: 40, 45, 50, 55, 60

Set B: 48, 49, 50, 51, 52

Which statement is true?

- **A.** The means are equal, and set B has the larger standard deviation.
- **B.** Set A has the larger mean and the larger standard deviation.
- **C.** The means are equal, and set A has the larger standard deviation.
- **D.** The means and the standard deviations are equal.

## 3. Margin of error

A random sample of 500 voters in one city found that 46% support a new park. The margin of error is 3%.

Which conclusion is best supported?

- **A.** Exactly 46% of the city's voters support the park.
- **B.** It is plausible that between 43% and 49% of the city's voters support the park.
- **C.** Between 43% and 49% of all voters in the country support the park.
- **D.** A larger sample would make the margin of error larger.

## 4. Given the sum

Two fair six-sided dice are rolled.

Given that the sum is 8, what is the probability that at least one die shows a 3?

- **A.** $\tfrac{2}{5}$
- **B.** $\tfrac{1}{18}$
- **C.** $\tfrac{1}{6}$
- **D.** $\tfrac{5}{36}$

## 5. Residual from a line of fit

A line of best fit for a scatterplot is

$$
y = 1.8x + 32
$$

One data point is $(25, 74)$. What is the residual for this point?

Answer: ______

---

<details>
<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. $20$ — Compare the sums**

$$
\begin{aligned} 5 \times 14 &= 70 \\ 6 \times 15 &= 90 \\ 90 - 70 &= \boxed{20} \end{aligned}
$$

**2. C — Same centre, different spread**

Both sets are symmetric about 50, so both means are 50. Set A's values lie 5 to 10 from 50; set B's lie 1 to 2 from 50. Set A is more spread out: **larger standard deviation**.

**3. B — Estimate ± margin, same population**

$46\% \pm 3\%$ gives 43% to 49%, and it describes the **city** (where the sample came from), not the country. A larger sample makes the margin smaller.

**4. A — List only the outcomes with sum 8**

Sum 8: (2,6), (3,5), (4,4), (5,3), (6,2). That is 5 outcomes.

With a 3: (3,5) and (5,3). That is 2 outcomes.

$$
P = \boxed{\tfrac{2}{5}}
$$

$\tfrac{1}{18}$ divides by all 36 outcomes, ignoring “given that”.

**5. $-3$ — Actual minus predicted**

$$
\begin{aligned} \hat{y} &= 1.8(25) + 32 \\ &= 77 \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned} \text{residual} &= 74 - 77 \\ &= \boxed{-3} \end{aligned}
$$

Negative: the line overestimates this point.

Turn means into sums, condition by shrinking the denominator, generalise only to the sampled population, and residual = actual − predicted.

</details>
