# SAT Math 8 — Ratios, Rates and Percentages Quiz

Five questions on ratios, rates and percentages. **Show your working before you choose or type an answer.**

---

## 1. A ratio changes

In a class of 36 students, the ratio of boys to girls is 4 : 5.

Four more boys join the class. What is the new ratio of boys to girls?

- **A.** 4 : 5
- **B.** 5 : 4
- **C.** 1 : 1
- **D.** 8 : 9

## 2. Square units

A rectangular floor is 4.5 m long and 3.2 m wide.

What is its area in square centimetres?

Answer: ______

## 3. Up then down

A price rises by 25%, then the new price falls by 20%.

What is the overall change?

- **A.** A 5% increase
- **B.** No change
- **C.** A 5% decrease
- **D.** A 45% increase

## 4. Pages per minute

A printer prints 18 pages in 45 seconds.

At this rate, how many minutes does it take to print 300 pages?

Answer: ______

## 5. A percent of a percent

In a survey of 400 people, 35% own a dog.

Of the dog owners, 40% also own a cat. How many people own both a dog and a cat?

- **A.** $140$
- **B.** $160$
- **C.** $300$
- **D.** $56$

---

<details>
<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. C — Find the counts first**

$$
\begin{aligned} \text{boys} &= 36 \cdot \tfrac{4}{9} \\ &= 16 \\ \text{girls} &= 36 \cdot \tfrac{5}{9} \\ &= 20 \end{aligned}
$$

After 4 boys join: 20 boys and 20 girls, so $\boxed{1 : 1}$. Adding 4 to the ratio parts gives 8 : 9, which is wrong.

**2. $144000$ — Square metres, then × 10,000**

$$
\begin{aligned} 4.5 \times 3.2 &= 14.4\ \text{m}^2 \\ 14.4 \times 10{,}000 &= 144{,}000\ \text{cm}^2 \end{aligned}
$$

$\times 100$ gives 1,440: one metre is 100 cm, but one square metre is $100^2$ square centimetres.

**3. B — Multiply the factors**

$$
\begin{aligned} 1.25 \times 0.80 &= 1.00 \end{aligned}
$$

The final price equals the original: **no change**. $25 - 20 = 5$ adds percentages of different amounts.

**4. $12.5$ — Time per page, then convert**

$$
\begin{aligned} 300 \times \frac{45\ \text{s}}{18} &= 750\ \text{s} \\ 750 \div 60 &= 12.5\ \text{min} \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boxed{12.5}
$$

**5. D — 40% of the dog owners, not of everyone**

$$
\begin{aligned} 0.35 \times 400 &= 140 \\ 0.40 \times 140 &= \boxed{56} \end{aligned}
$$

160 is 40% of all 400 people.

Write every percent as a multiplier, multiply successive changes, divide to undo one, and square the factor for square units.

</details>
