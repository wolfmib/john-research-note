## Homework Q4 · Multiply first

### 1. Multiply, then subtract

Work out $\dfrac{5}{6}-\dfrac{2}{3}\times\dfrac{1}{4}$.

Answer: ______

### 2. Different denominators

Answer: ______

## Homework Q12 · Fraction solution

### 9. Simplify the coefficient

Solve $\dfrac{3x}{6}+\dfrac{1}{3}=3-\dfrac{x}{3}$.

Write your answer in simplest form.

Answer: ______

### 10. Collect fractional terms

Solve $\dfrac{7x}{14}+\dfrac{1}{6}=2-\dfrac{x}{3}$.

Write your answer in simplest form.

Answer: ______

<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. $\frac{2}{3}$** — Multiply before subtracting.

$$
\frac{2}{3}\times\frac{1}{4}=\frac{1}{6}
$$

$$
\frac{5}{6}-\frac{1}{6}=\frac{4}{6}
$$

$$
\frac{4}{6}=\boxed{\frac{2}{3}}
$$

Subtracting first would change the order of operations.

**2. $\frac{23}{40}$** — Multiply first, then use a common denominator.

$$
\frac{3}{4}\times\frac{2}{5}=\frac{3}{10}
$$

$$
\frac{7}{8}-\frac{3}{10}=\frac{35}{40}-\frac{12}{40}
$$

$$
\frac{35}{40}-\frac{12}{40}=\boxed{\frac{23}{40}}
$$

Subtracting the denominators does not give a common unit.

**3. $\frac{1}{6}$** — Divide the total by the number of glasses.

$$
\frac{2}{3}\div4=\frac{2}{3}\times\frac{1}{4}
$$

$$
\frac{2}{3}\times\frac{1}{4}=\frac{2}{12}
$$

$$
\frac{2}{12}=\boxed{\frac{1}{6}}\text{ litre}
$$

Multiplying by 4 would make the quantity larger instead of sharing it.

**4. $\frac{5}{24}$** — Dividing by 4 means multiplying by its reciprocal.

$$
\frac{5}{6}\div4=\frac{5}{6}\times\frac{1}{4}
$$

$$
\frac{5}{6}\times\frac{1}{4}=\boxed{\frac{5}{24}}\text{ litre}
$$

Keep the numerator 5; sharing into four cups multiplies the denominator by 4.

**5. $\frac{7}{10}$** — Multiply first.

$$
\frac{3}{5}\times\frac{5}{12}=\frac{1}{4}
$$

$$
\frac{7}{10}-\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{4}=\boxed{\frac{7}{10}}
$$

The subtraction and addition of the same quarter cancel; the multiplication must be done first.

**6. $\frac{19}{24}$** — Multiply first, then write the remaining fractions in twenty-fourths.

$$
\frac{3}{4}\times\frac{2}{9}=\frac{1}{6}
$$

$$
\frac{7}{8}-\frac{1}{6}+\frac{1}{12}=\frac{21}{24}-\frac{4}{24}+\frac{2}{24}
$$

$$
\frac{21}{24}-\frac{4}{24}+\frac{2}{24}=\boxed{\frac{19}{24}}
$$

Keep the minus sign with the product; only the last term is added.

**7. $14$** — Subtract $x/7$ from both sides and add 2 to both sides.

$$
\frac{4x}{7}-\frac{x}{7}=4+2
$$

$$
\frac{3x}{7}=6
$$

$$
3x=42
$$

$$
x=\boxed{14}
$$

Dividing by 7 again would make the fraction smaller; multiply both sides by 7 to clear it.

**8. $9$** — Subtract $x/6$ from both sides and add 1 to both sides.

$$
\frac{5x}{6}-\frac{x}{6}=5+1
$$

$$
\frac{4x}{6}=6
$$

$$
4x=36
$$

$$
x=\boxed{9}
$$

The minus 1 is undone by adding 1 to both sides.

**9. $\frac{16}{5}$** — Simplify the first fraction.

$$
\frac{x}{2}+\frac{1}{3}=3-\frac{x}{3}
$$

Add $x/3$ and subtract $1/3$ on both sides.

$$
\frac{x}{2}+\frac{x}{3}=3-\frac{1}{3}
$$

$$
\frac{5x}{6}=\frac{8}{3}
$$

$$
5x=16
$$

$$
x=\boxed{\frac{16}{5}}
$$

To undo multiplication by $5/6$, multiply by $6/5$.

**10. $\frac{11}{5}$** — Simplify the first fraction.

$$
\frac{x}{2}+\frac{1}{6}=2-\frac{x}{3}
$$

Add $x/3$ and subtract $1/6$ on both sides.

$$
\frac{x}{2}+\frac{x}{3}=2-\frac{1}{6}
$$

$$
\frac{5x}{6}=\frac{11}{6}
$$

$$
5x=11
$$

$$
x=\boxed{\frac{11}{5}}
$$

Keep the coefficient numerator 5 when dividing to find $x$; the denominator 6 has already been cleared.
