# AP Precalculus 2.3 — Exponential Functions

## 1. Increasing or decreasing?

Consider:

$$
f(x)=-4\cdot3^x
$$

Which statement is correct?

- **A.** The function increases and stays positive.
- **B.** The function decreases and stays positive.
- **C.** The function increases and stays negative.
- **D.** The function decreases and stays negative.

## 2. End behavior

Consider:

$$
g(x)=5\left(\frac12\right)^x
$$

What happens to $g(x)$ at each end of the graph?

- **A.** As $x\to-\infty$, $g(x)\to0$; as $x\to+\infty$, $g(x)\to+\infty$.
- **B.** As $x\to-\infty$, $g(x)\to+\infty$; as $x\to+\infty$, $g(x)\to0$.
- **C.** As $x\to-\infty$, $g(x)\to-\infty$; as $x\to+\infty$, $g(x)\to0$.
- **D.** As $x\to-\infty$, $g(x)\to0$; as $x\to+\infty$, $g(x)\to-\infty$.

## 3. Graph features

Consider:

$$
h(x)=-2\left(\frac13\right)^x
$$

Which option correctly identifies its **$y$-intercept, range, and horizontal asymptote**?

| Option | $y$-intercept | Range | Horizontal asymptote |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | $(0,-2)$ | $(-\infty,0)$ | $y=0$ |
| B | $(0,-2)$ | $(-\infty,-2)$ | $y=-2$ |
| C | $(0,2)$ | $(0,\infty)$ | $y=0$ |
| D | $(0,-2)$ | $(-\infty,0]$ | $y=0$ |

---

<details>
<summary><b>Answers / Teacher Reference</b></summary>

**1. D** — Check successive values:

$$
f(0)=-4,\qquad f(1)=-12,\qquad f(2)=-36
$$

The values become more negative, so the function **decreases**. Because $3^x>0$, its output is always negative.

**2. B** — A base between $0$ and $1$ produces decay when the coefficient is positive:

$$
g(-3)=40,\qquad g(0)=5,\qquad g(3)=\frac58
$$

Moving right, the curve approaches $0$ **from above**. Moving left, it grows without bound.

**3. A** — At $x=0$:

$$
h(0)=-2\left(\frac13\right)^0=-2
$$

The outputs are always negative and approach—but never reach—$0$. Therefore:

$$
\boxed{\text{Range: }(-\infty,0),\qquad \text{horizontal asymptote: }y=0}
$$

</details>
