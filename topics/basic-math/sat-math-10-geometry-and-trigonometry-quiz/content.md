# SAT Math 10 — Geometry and Trigonometry Quiz

Five questions on geometry and trigonometry. **Show your working before you choose or type an answer.**

---

## 1. Complementary angles

$$
\cos(x^\circ) = \sin(52^\circ)
$$

$0 < x < 90$. What is the value of $x$?

- **A.** $52$
- **B.** $128$
- **C.** $142$
- **D.** $38$

## 2. Arc length in radians

A circle has radius 9. A central angle of $\tfrac{2\pi}{3}$ radians cuts off an arc of length $k\pi$.

What is the value of $k$?

Answer: ______

## 3. Similar cones

Two cones are similar. Their heights are 4 cm and 10 cm.

The smaller cone has a volume of $24\ \text{cm}^3$. What is the volume of the larger cone?

- **A.** $60\ \text{cm}^3$
- **B.** $150\ \text{cm}^3$
- **C.** $375\ \text{cm}^3$
- **D.** $240\ \text{cm}^3$

## 4. Equilateral triangle area

An equilateral triangle has sides of length 10.

What is its area?

- **A.** $50$
- **B.** $25\sqrt{3}$
- **C.** $50\sqrt{3}$
- **D.** $100$

## 5. Parallel lines

Lines $\ell$ and $m$ are parallel. A transversal crosses them.

Two co-interior (same-side interior) angles measure $(3x + 10)^\circ$ and $(2x - 5)^\circ$.

What is the value of $x$?

Answer: ______

---

<details>
<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. D — sin x° = cos(90° − x°)**

$$
\begin{aligned} \sin 52^\circ &= \cos(90^\circ - 52^\circ) \\ &= \cos 38^\circ \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boxed{x = 38}
$$

**2. $6$ — Arc length = rθ**

$$
\begin{aligned} s &= 9 \cdot \tfrac{2\pi}{3} \\ &= 6\pi \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boxed{k = 6}
$$

**3. C — Volume scales by k³**

$$
\begin{aligned} k &= \tfrac{10}{4} \\ &= 2.5 \\ k^3 &= 15.625 \\ 24 \times 15.625 &= \boxed{375} \end{aligned}
$$

60 scales by $k$ and 150 by $k^2$: those are the rules for length and area.

**4. B — The height splits it into two 30°–60°–90° triangles**

Half the base is 5, so the height is $5\sqrt{3}$.

$$
\begin{aligned} \text{Area} &= \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot 10 \cdot 5\sqrt{3} \\ &= \boxed{25\sqrt{3}} \end{aligned}
$$

$50\sqrt{3}$ forgets the $\tfrac{1}{2}$.

**5. $35$ — Co-interior angles add to 180°**

$$
\begin{aligned} (3x + 10) + (2x - 5) &= 180 \\ 5x + 5 &= 180 \\ x &= \boxed{35} \end{aligned}
$$

Setting the two angles equal ($x = -15$) is the rule for alternate angles, and gives a negative angle.

Use angle facts and similarity ratios (k, k², k³), SOH CAH TOA with the special triangles, and complete the square to read a circle.

</details>
