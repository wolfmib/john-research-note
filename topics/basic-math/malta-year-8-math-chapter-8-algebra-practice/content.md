<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. $7$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 4x &= 28 \\ x &= 28\div4 \\ x &= \boxed{7} \end{aligned}
$$

$24$ takes 4 away; $4x$ means $4\times x$, so divide.

**2. $6$**

$$
\begin{aligned} x + 9 &= 15 \\ x &= 15 - 9 \\ x &= \boxed{6} \end{aligned}
$$

$24$ adds the 9 instead of taking it away.

**3. $15$**

$$
\begin{aligned} x - 4 &= 11 \\ x &= 11 + 4 \\ x &= \boxed{15} \end{aligned}
$$

$7$ takes 4 away again; undo $-4$ with $+4$.

**4. $15$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{x}{3} &= 5 \\ x &= 5\times3 \\ x &= \boxed{15} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{5}{3}$ divides again; undo $\div3$ with $\times3$.

**5. $-5$**

$$
\begin{aligned} x + 8 &= 3 \\ x &= 3 - 8 \\ x &= \boxed{-5} \end{aligned}
$$

- $5$ forgets the sign: $3 - 8$ is below zero.
- Use the ± button.

**6. $6$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 3x - 7 &= 11 \\ 3x &= 11 + 7 \\ 3x &= 18 \\ x &= \boxed{6} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{4}{3}$ takes the 7 away; $-7$ moves across as $+7$.

**7. $7$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 2x + 5 &= 19 \\ 2x &= 19 - 5 \\ 2x &= 14 \\ x &= \boxed{7} \end{aligned}
$$

$12$ divides 24 by 2: the 5 moves across as $-5$.

**8. $5$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 5x + 3 &= 28 \\ 5x &= 28 - 3 \\ 5x &= 25 \\ x &= \boxed{5} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{31}{5}$ adds the 3; it moves across as $-3$.

**9. $8$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 4x - 9 &= 23 \\ 4x &= 23 + 9 \\ 4x &= 32 \\ x &= \boxed{8} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{7}{2}$ takes the 9 away; $-9$ moves across as $+9$.

**10. $-1$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 6x + 10 &= 4 \\ 6x &= 4 - 10 \\ 6x &= -6 \\ x &= \boxed{-1} \end{aligned}
$$

$1$ loses the minus: $4 - 10 = -6$.

**11. $6$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 5x + 4 &= x + 16 + 2x \\ 5x - x - 2x &= 16 - 4 \\ 2x &= 12 \\ x &= \boxed{6} \end{aligned}
$$

Trick 2: $+x$ and $+2x$ move left as $-x$ and $-2x$; $+4$ moves right as $-4$.

**12. $4$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 7x - 2 &= 3x + 10 + x \\ 7x - 3x - x &= 10 + 2 \\ 3x &= 12 \\ x &= \boxed{4} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{8}{3}$ forgets to flip: $-2$ moves right as $+2$.

**13. $-8$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 4x + 9 &= 2x + 1 + x \\ 4x - 2x - x &= 1 - 9 \\ x &= \boxed{-8} \end{aligned}
$$

- $8$ loses the minus: $1 - 9 = -8$.
- Use the ± button.

**14. $8$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 6x - 5 &= 2x + 11 + 2x \\ 6x - 2x - 2x &= 11 + 5 \\ 2x &= 16 \\ x &= \boxed{8} \end{aligned}
$$

$3$ forgets to flip $-5$ into $+5$.

**15. $-5$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 9 + 3x &= x + 4 + x \\ 3x - x - x &= 4 - 9 \\ x &= \boxed{-5} \end{aligned}
$$

$13$ adds the 9; it moves across as $-9$.

**16. $\frac{9}{2}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 8x - 3 &= 5x + 6 + x \\ 8x - 5x - x &= 6 + 3 \\ 2x &= 9 \\ x &= \boxed{\frac{9}{2}} \end{aligned}
$$

- $9\div2$ is not a whole number: the answer is $\dfrac{9}{2}$.
- Type 9 on top and 2 below.

**17. $4$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 2x + 7 &= 15 - x + 4 \\ 2x + x &= 15 + 4 - 7 \\ 3x &= 12 \\ x &= \boxed{4} \end{aligned}
$$

$-x$ moves left as $+x$, so $2x + x = 3x$.

**18. $3$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 10x - 1 &= 3x + 2x + 14 \\ 10x - 3x - 2x &= 14 + 1 \\ 5x &= 15 \\ x &= \boxed{3} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{13}{5}$ forgets to flip $-1$ into $+1$.

**19. $4$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 3x + 2 &= 26 - 2x - 4 \\ 3x + 2x &= 26 - 4 - 2 \\ 5x &= 20 \\ x &= \boxed{4} \end{aligned}
$$

$-2x$ moves left as $+2x$; $20$ is $5x$, not $x$.

**20. $\frac{7}{2}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 5x - 8 &= x + 2x - 1 \\ 5x - x - 2x &= -1 + 8 \\ 2x &= 7 \\ x &= \boxed{\frac{7}{2}} \end{aligned}
$$

$x = \dfrac{7}{2}$: type 7 on top and 2 below.

**21. $5$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{2x}{4} + \frac{3}{6} &= 8 - x \\ \frac{x}{2} + \frac{1}{2} &= 8 - x \\ \frac{x}{2} + x &= 8 - \frac{1}{2} \\ \frac{3x}{2} &= \frac{15}{2} \\ x &= \boxed{5} \end{aligned}
$$

- Simplify first: $\dfrac{2x}{4} = \dfrac{x}{2}$ and $\dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
- Then $3x = 15$.

**22. $\frac{11}{2}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{2x}{2} + \frac{3}{6} &= 17 - 2x \\ x + \frac{1}{2} &= 17 - 2x \\ x + 2x &= 17 - \frac{1}{2} \\ 3x &= \frac{33}{2} \\ x &= \boxed{\frac{11}{2}} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{33}{2}\div3 = \dfrac{11}{2}$: type 11 on top and 2 below.

**23. $8$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{x}{2} + 3 &= \frac{x}{4} + 5 \\ \frac{x}{2} - \frac{x}{4} &= 5 - 3 \\ \frac{x}{4} &= 2 \\ x &= \boxed{8} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{x}{2} - \dfrac{x}{4} = \dfrac{2x}{4} - \dfrac{x}{4} = \dfrac{x}{4}$.

**24. $9$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{x}{3} + \frac{1}{2} &= \frac{7}{2} \\ \frac{x}{3} &= \frac{7}{2} - \frac{1}{2} \\ \frac{x}{3} &= 3 \\ x &= \boxed{9} \end{aligned}
$$

$3$ forgets the last step: $\dfrac{x}{3} = 3$ means $x = 3\times3 = 9$.

**25. $10$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{3x}{5} - 1 &= \frac{x}{5} + 3 \\ \frac{3x}{5} - \frac{x}{5} &= 3 + 1 \\ \frac{2x}{5} &= 4 \\ 2x &= 20 \\ x &= \boxed{10} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{2x}{5} = 4$: multiply by 5, then divide by 2.

**26. $12$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{x}{2} + \frac{x}{3} &= 10 \\ \frac{3x}{6} + \frac{2x}{6} &= 10 \\ \frac{5x}{6} &= 10 \\ 5x &= 60 \\ x &= \boxed{12} \end{aligned}
$$

$50$ adds the bottoms ($\dfrac{x}{5}$); use sixths.

**27. $3$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{4x}{6} + \frac{1}{3} &= x - \frac{2}{3} \\ \frac{2x}{3} + \frac{1}{3} &= x - \frac{2}{3} \\ \frac{1}{3} + \frac{2}{3} &= x - \frac{2x}{3} \\ 1 &= \frac{x}{3} \\ x &= \boxed{3} \end{aligned}
$$

Here the $x$ terms go right and the numbers go left; Trick 2 works both ways.

**28. $5$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{x}{4} - \frac{1}{2} &= 2 - \frac{x}{4} \\ \frac{x}{4} + \frac{x}{4} &= 2 + \frac{1}{2} \\ \frac{x}{2} &= \frac{5}{2} \\ x &= \boxed{5} \end{aligned}
$$

$-\dfrac{x}{4}$ moves left as $+\dfrac{x}{4}$.

**29. $6$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{3x}{9} + \frac{2}{4} &= \frac{x}{6} + \frac{3}{2} \\ \frac{x}{3} + \frac{1}{2} &= \frac{x}{6} + \frac{3}{2} \\ \frac{x}{3} - \frac{x}{6} &= \frac{3}{2} - \frac{1}{2} \\ \frac{x}{6} &= 1 \\ x &= \boxed{6} \end{aligned}
$$

Simplify first: $\dfrac{3x}{9} = \dfrac{x}{3}$, $\dfrac{2}{4} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

**30. $\frac{7}{3}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{5x}{10} + \frac{1}{4} &= 2 - \frac{x}{4} \\ \frac{x}{2} + \frac{1}{4} &= 2 - \frac{x}{4} \\ \frac{x}{2} + \frac{x}{4} &= 2 - \frac{1}{4} \\ \frac{3x}{4} &= \frac{7}{4} \\ 3x &= 7 \\ x &= \boxed{\frac{7}{3}} \end{aligned}
$$

$3x = 7$ gives $x = \dfrac{7}{3}$: type 7 on top and 3 below.

- Keep the balance: do the same to both sides.
- Trick 2: a term that moves across = flips its sign.
- A number that multiplies x moves across as ÷.
- Simplify the fractions first.
