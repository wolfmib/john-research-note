# Malta Year 8 Math Chapter 3 — Angles Recap

**Quick recap:** straight line $180^\circ$; around a point $360^\circ$; triangle $180^\circ$; opposite, corresponding (F) and alternate (Z) angles are equal; co-interior (C) angles add up to $180^\circ$.

---

## 12. 3.5 · Alternate angles

Alternate angles (a **Z** shape) on parallel lines …

- **A.** are equal
- **B.** add up to $180^\circ$
- **C.** add up to $360^\circ$
- **D.** add up to $90^\circ$

---

<details>
<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. C — Between $90^\circ$ and $180^\circ$**

$$
135^\circ \to \boxed{\text{obtuse}}
$$

Reflex angles start after $180^\circ$.

**2. D — Between $180^\circ$ and $360^\circ$**

$$
\boxed{250^\circ} \to \text{reflex}
$$

$180^\circ$ is a straight angle, and $175^\circ$ is obtuse.

**3. $62^\circ$**

$$
x = 180^\circ - 118^\circ = \boxed{62^\circ}
$$

$242$ uses $360^\circ$, which is for angles around a point.

**4. $65^\circ$** — $45^\circ + 70^\circ = 115^\circ$:

$$
x = 180^\circ - 115^\circ = \boxed{65^\circ}
$$

$245$ uses $360^\circ$ instead of $180^\circ$.

**5. $125^\circ$** — $140^\circ + 95^\circ = 235^\circ$:

$$
x = 360^\circ - 235^\circ = \boxed{125^\circ}
$$

Using $180^\circ$ gives a negative angle — a sign the wrong total was used.

**6. $122^\circ$** — Neighbouring angles sit on a straight line:

$$
180^\circ - 58^\circ = \boxed{122^\circ}
$$

$58^\circ$ is the **opposite** angle, not the neighbour.

**7. $90^\circ$** — $75^\circ + 105^\circ + 90^\circ = 270^\circ$:

$$
x = 360^\circ - 270^\circ = \boxed{90^\circ}
$$

**8. $65^\circ$** — $48^\circ + 67^\circ = 115^\circ$:

$$
180^\circ - 115^\circ = \boxed{65^\circ}
$$

$245$ uses $360^\circ$ — a triangle's angles add up to $180^\circ$.

**9. $75^\circ$**

$$
(180^\circ - 30^\circ)\div2 = 150^\circ\div2 = \boxed{75^\circ}
$$

$150$ forgets to share between the two base angles.

**10. $117^\circ$**

$$
55^\circ + 62^\circ = \boxed{117^\circ}
$$

$63$ is the third **inside** angle ($180^\circ - 117^\circ$), next to the exterior angle on the straight line.

**11. $72^\circ$**

$$
180^\circ - 108^\circ = \boxed{72^\circ}
$$

$108$ would be right for corresponding or alternate angles, which are equal.

**12. A — Z angles match**

$$
\boxed{\text{equal}}
$$

Adding up to $180^\circ$ is the rule for co-interior angles (a **C** shape).

Line 180°, point 360°, triangle 180°; opposite, corresponding and alternate angles are equal; co-interior angles add up to 180°; an exterior angle equals the two opposite inside angles.

</details>
