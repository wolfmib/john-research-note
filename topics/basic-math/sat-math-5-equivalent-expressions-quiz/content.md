# SAT Math 5 — Equivalent Expressions Quiz

Five questions on equivalent expressions. **Show your working before you choose or type an answer.**

---

## 1. Exponent rules

For $x > 0$, which expression is equivalent to

$$
\frac{(2x^3)^2 \cdot x^{-1}}{8x^2}\,?
$$

- **A.** $\dfrac{x^2}{2}$
- **B.** $2x^3$
- **C.** $\dfrac{x^3}{2}$
- **D.** $\dfrac{x^4}{4}$

## 2. Subtract two fractions

For $x \ne 1$ and $x \ne -1$, which is equivalent to

$$
\frac{2}{x - 1} - \frac{1}{x + 1}\,?
$$

- **A.** $\dfrac{1}{x^2 - 1}$
- **B.** $\dfrac{x + 3}{x^2 - 1}$
- **C.** $\dfrac{x + 1}{x^2 - 1}$
- **D.** $\dfrac{3x + 1}{x^2 - 1}$

## 3. Fractional exponents

For $x > 0$, which is equivalent to

$$
\sqrt[3]{x^2} \cdot \sqrt{x}\,?
$$

- **A.** $x^{1/3}$
- **B.** $x^{3/5}$
- **C.** $x^{2/5}$
- **D.** $x^{7/6}$

## 4. Difference of squares

$$
\begin{aligned} x - y &= 4 \\ x + y &= 9 \end{aligned}
$$

What is the value of $x^2 - y^2$?

Answer: ______

## 5. Complete the square

Which expression is equivalent to $x^2 + 10x + 31$?

- **A.** $(x + 5)^2 + 6$
- **B.** $(x + 5)^2 + 31$
- **C.** $(x + 10)^2 - 69$
- **D.** $(x + 5)^2 - 6$

---

<details>
<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. C — Square everything in the bracket**

$$
\begin{aligned} (2x^3)^2 &= 4x^6 \\ 4x^6 \cdot x^{-1} &= 4x^5 \\ \frac{4x^5}{8x^2} &= \frac{x^3}{2} \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boxed{\dfrac{x^3}{2}}
$$

$2x^3$ forgets to square the 2.

**2. B — Common denominator (x − 1)(x + 1)**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{2(x + 1) - (x - 1)}{(x - 1)(x + 1)} &= \frac{2x + 2 - x + 1}{x^2 - 1} \\ &= \frac{x + 3}{x^2 - 1} \end{aligned}
$$

$x + 1$ on top comes from $-(x - 1) = -x - 1$: the minus must reach the $-1$ too.

**3. D — Write the roots as powers, then add**

$$
\begin{aligned} \sqrt[3]{x^2} &= x^{2/3} \\ \sqrt{x} &= x^{1/2} \\ x^{2/3} \cdot x^{1/2} &= x^{2/3 + 1/2} \\ &= x^{7/6} \end{aligned}
$$

$x^{1/3}$ multiplies the exponents instead of adding them.

**4. $36$ — No need to find x and y**

$$
\begin{aligned} x^2 - y^2 &= (x - y)(x + y) \\ &= 4 \cdot 9 \\ &= \boxed{36} \end{aligned}
$$

**5. A — Half of 10 is 5**

$$
\begin{aligned} (x + 5)^2 &= x^2 + 10x + 25 \\ x^2 + 10x + 31 &= (x + 5)^2 + 6 \end{aligned}
$$

$(x + 5)^2 + 31$ would give a constant of 56.

Expand or factor carefully, use the exponent rules, and when two polynomials are equal for all x, match their coefficients.

</details>
