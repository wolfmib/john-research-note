# SAT Math 4 — Linear Inequalities Quiz

Five questions on linear inequalities. **Show your working before you choose or type an answer.**

---

## 1. Flip the sign

$$
-3(x - 4) \ge 2x + 2
$$

Which describes all solutions?

- **A.** $x \ge 2$
- **B.** $x \le 2$
- **C.** $x \le -2$
- **D.** $x \ge -2$

## 2. Least score needed

A student scored 78, 85 and 90 on three tests.

What is the least score on the fourth test that makes the mean of the four tests at least 85?

Answer: ______

## 3. Write the inequality

A bakery sells pastizzi for €0.60 each and qassatat for €0.90 each.

It must earn at least €270 in a day. It sells $p$ pastizzi and $q$ qassatat.

Which inequality models this?

- **A.** $0.6p + 0.9q \le 270$
- **B.** $0.9p + 0.6q \ge 270$
- **C.** $p + q \ge 270$
- **D.** $0.6p + 0.9q \ge 270$

## 4. Fractions in an inequality

$$
\frac{x}{3} - \frac{1}{2} < \frac{x}{4}
$$

Which describes all solutions?

- **A.** $x < 6$
- **B.** $x > 6$
- **C.** $x < \tfrac{1}{2}$
- **D.** $x < 18$

## 5. Count the integers

$$
-7 < 2x + 3 \le 11
$$

How many integers $x$ satisfy the inequality?

Answer: ______

---

<details>
<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. B — Collect x on the side where it stays positive**

$$
\begin{aligned} -3x + 12 &\ge 2x + 2 \\ 10 &\ge 5x \\ 2 &\ge x \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boxed{x \le 2}
$$

If you divide by $-5$ instead, remember to flip: $x \le 2$ again, not $x \ge 2$.

**2. $87$ — Mean at least 85 means total at least 340**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{78 + 85 + 90 + s}{4} &\ge 85 \\ 253 + s &\ge 340 \\ s &\ge 87 \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boxed{87}
$$

**3. D — Price times number, then 'at least'**

Money from pastizzi: $0.6p$. Money from qassatat: $0.9q$.

“At least €270” is $\ge$:

$$
\boxed{0.6p + 0.9q \ge 270}
$$

**4. A — Multiply by 12 (positive, so no flip)**

$$
\begin{aligned} 4x - 6 &< 3x \\ x &< 6 \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boxed{x < 6}
$$

**5. $9$ — Do the same to all three parts**

$$
\begin{aligned} -10 &< 2x \le 8 \\ -5 &< x \le 4 \end{aligned}
$$

The integers are $-4, -3, \ldots, 4$: $\boxed{9}$ of them. $-5$ is not included (strict $<$); 4 is.

Solve like an equation, flip the sign for a negative multiplier, and round whole-number answers in the direction the limit allows.

</details>
