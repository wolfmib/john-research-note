# SAT Math 8 — Ratios, Rates and Percentages

**SAT domain:** Problem-Solving and Data Analysis (5–7 of the 44 math questions).

**Key ideas**
- A proportion $\dfrac{a}{b} = \dfrac{c}{d}$ is solved by cross-multiplying.
- Convert units by multiplying by fractions equal to 1:
  - $\dfrac{1\ \text{km}}{1000\ \text{m}}$, $\dfrac{60\ \text{s}}{1\ \text{min}}$
- Squared units square the factor:
  - $1\ \text{m}^2 = 100^2\ \text{cm}^2 = 10{,}000\ \text{cm}^2$
- Percent change:
  - $\dfrac{\text{new} - \text{old}}{\text{old}} \times 100\%$
- Successive changes **multiply**: $+20\%$ then $-20\%$ is a 4% decrease.
  - $1.2 \times 0.8 = 0.96$
- Reverse percent: after a change by factor $m$, the original is $\dfrac{\text{new}}{m}$.

**Watch out**
- A discount of 15% is not undone by adding 15% back.

## 1. Exam 1 — Price before the discount

A jacket costs €68 after a 15% discount.

What was the price before the discount?

**A.** €80

**B.** €78.20

**C.** €57.80

**D.** €83

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

After a 15% discount the price is 85% of the original:

$$
0.85p = 68
$$

$$
\begin{aligned} p &= \frac{68}{0.85} \\ &= 80 \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boxed{€80}
$$

**Answer: A** — €78.20 adds 15% of €68: the 15% belongs to the original price, not the new one.

</details>

## 2. Exam 2 — Fuel cost

A car uses 6.5 litres of fuel per 100 km. Fuel costs €1.60 per litre.

How many euros does the fuel cost for a 340 km trip? (Type the answer.)

<details>
<summary><b>Working</b></summary>

Fuel used:

$$
\begin{aligned} 340\ \text{km} \times \frac{6.5\ \text{L}}{100\ \text{km}} &= 22.1\ \text{L} \end{aligned}
$$

Cost:

$$
\begin{aligned} 22.1\ \text{L} \times \frac{€1.60}{1\ \text{L}} &= €35.36 \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\boxed{35.36}
$$

</details>
