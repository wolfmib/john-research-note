# Malta Year 8 Math Chapter 6 — Percentages Practice

**Three levels:**

- Easy: percent, fraction, decimal and the quick percentages
- Medium: build from 10%, 5% and 1%; a score as a percentage
- Hard: sale prices, VAT, rises and falls

- **Answers:** type the number only (no € or % sign).
- A fraction goes in two boxes, in its simplest form.

<details>
<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. $0.3$**

$$
30\div100 = \boxed{0.3}
$$

$3$ is $300\%$; divide by 100, not by 10.

**2. $45$**

$$
0.45\times100 = \boxed{45}\%
$$

$4.5$ moves the digits only one place.

**3. $75$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{3}{4} &= \frac{75}{100} \\ &= \boxed{75}\% \end{aligned}
$$

$34$ just joins the digits; make the bottom 100.

**4. $\frac{2}{5}$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 40\% &= \frac{40}{100} \\ &= \boxed{\frac{2}{5}} \end{aligned}
$$

$\dfrac{40}{100}$ is right but not simplest: divide top and bottom by 20.

**5. $32$**

$$
64\div2 = \boxed{32}
$$

$128$ doubles instead of halving.

**6. $9$**

$$
90\div10 = \boxed{9}
$$

$0.9$ divides by 100; $10\%$ divides by 10.

**7. $11$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 44\div2 &= 22 \\ 22\div2 &= \boxed{11} \end{aligned}
$$

$22$ is only $50\%$; halve again.

**8. $5$**

$$
500\div100 = \boxed{5}
$$

$50$ is $10\%$.

**9. $7$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 10\% &= 3.5 \\ 20\% &= 2\times3.5 \\ &= \boxed{7} \end{aligned}
$$

$3.5$ is only $10\%$; double it.

**10. $15$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 25\% &= 5 \\ 75\% &= 3\times5 \\ &= \boxed{15} \end{aligned}
$$

$5$ is $25\%$; $75\%$ is three of those.

**11. $9$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 10\% &= 6 \\ 5\% &= 3 \\ 6 + 3 &= \boxed{9} \end{aligned}
$$

$6$ is only the $10\%$; add the $5\%$.

**12. $28$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 10\% &= 8 \\ 30\% &= 24 \\ 5\% &= 4 \\ 24 + 4 &= \boxed{28} \end{aligned}
$$

$24$ forgets the $5\%$.

**13. $6$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 10\% &= 5 \\ 1\% &= 0.5 \\ 5 + 0.5 + 0.5 &= \boxed{6} \end{aligned}
$$

$5.5$ adds only one $1\%$; $12\% = 10\% + 1\% + 1\%$.

**14. $9$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 10\% &= 2 \\ 40\% &= 8 \\ 5\% &= 1 \\ 8 + 1 &= \boxed{9} \end{aligned}
$$

$8$ forgets the $5\%$.

**15. $90$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{18}{20} &= \frac{90}{100} \\ &= \boxed{90}\% \end{aligned}
$$

$18\%$ forgets that the whole is 20, not 100.

**16. $56$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{14}{25} &= \frac{56}{100} \\ &= \boxed{56}\% \end{aligned}
$$

$\times4$ top and bottom makes the bottom 100.

**17. $75$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{9}{12} &= \frac{3}{4} \\ &= \frac{75}{100} \\ &= \boxed{75}\% \end{aligned}
$$

Simplify first: $\dfrac{9}{12} = \dfrac{3}{4}$.

**18. $9$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 1\% &= 1.5 \\ 6\% &= 6\times1.5 \\ &= \boxed{9} \end{aligned}
$$

$1.5$ is only $1\%$.

**19. $80$**

$$
\begin{aligned} \frac{32}{40} &= \frac{8}{10} \\ &= \boxed{80}\% \end{aligned}
$$

$32\%$ forgets that the whole is 40.

**20. $35$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 10\% &= 20 \\ 5\% &= 10 \\ 2.5\% &= 5 \\ 20 + 10 + 5 &= \boxed{35} \end{aligned}
$$

$2.5\%$ is half of $5\%$.

**21. $60$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 25\% \text{ of } 80 &= 20 \\ 80 - 20 &= \boxed{60} \end{aligned}
$$

$20$ is the money off, not the new price.

**22. $59$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 10\% &= 5 \\ 8\% &= 4 \\ 18\% &= 9 \\ 50 + 9 &= \boxed{59} \end{aligned}
$$

$9$ is only the VAT; add it on.

**23. $102$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 10\% &= 12 \\ 5\% &= 6 \\ 15\% &= 18 \\ 120 - 18 &= \boxed{102} \end{aligned}
$$

$138$ adds the $15\%$ on instead of taking it off.

**24. $630$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 5\% \text{ of } 600 &= 30 \\ 600 + 30 &= \boxed{630} \end{aligned}
$$

$605$ adds 5 euros, not $5\%$.

**25. $300$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 20\% \text{ of } 250 &= 50 \\ 250 + 50 &= \boxed{300} \end{aligned}
$$

$270$ adds 20 people, not $20\%$.

**26. $168$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 10\% &= 24 \\ 30\% &= 72 \\ 240 - 72 &= \boxed{168} \end{aligned}
$$

$72$ is the money off.

**27. $33$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 10\% \text{ of } 30 &= 3 \\ 30 + 3 &= \boxed{33} \end{aligned}
$$

$3$ is only the tip.

**28. $36$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 10\% &= 4.5 \\ 20\% &= 9 \\ 45 - 9 &= \boxed{36} \end{aligned}
$$

$25$ takes away 20 euros, not $20\%$.

**29. $760$**

$$
\begin{aligned} 10\% &= 80 \\ 5\% &= 40 \\ 800 - 40 &= \boxed{760} \end{aligned}
$$

$795$ takes away 5 people, not $5\%$.

**30. $80$**

$$
64\times1.25 = \boxed{80}
$$

$64\times0.25 = 16$ is only the rise; $\times1.25$ gives the new price.

- Per cent means out of 100.
- 10%: divide by 10. 5% is half of 10%. 1%: divide by 100.
- Sale: take the percentage off. VAT or a rise: add it on.
