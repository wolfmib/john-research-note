# AP Precalculus 2.2 — Changes in Linear and Exponential Functions

**📱 [Take this quiz on your phone](https://wolfmib.github.io/john-research-note/topics/basic-math/ap-precalculus-2-2-changes-in-linear-and-exponential-functions/quiz.html)** — 3 questions, Practice or Test mode.

## 1. Identify the type of change

The table shows two functions:

| $x$ | $f(x)$ | $g(x)$ |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0 | 3 | 3 |
| 1 | 6 | 6 |
| 2 | 9 | 12 |
| 3 | 12 | 24 |

Which statement correctly describes their changes?

- **A.** Both functions have a constant difference.
- **B.** $f$ has a constant difference of $3$; $g$ has a constant ratio of $2$.
- **C.** $f$ has a constant ratio of $3$; $g$ has a constant difference of $2$.
- **D.** Both functions have a constant ratio.

## 2. Linear change over an interval

A linear function satisfies $f(2)=7$ and $f(5)=19$. What is $f(3.5)$?

- **A.** $9$
- **B.** $11$
- **C.** $13$
- **D.** $15$

## 3. Exponential change over an interval

An exponential function satisfies $g(1)=6$ and $g(3)=24$. Its base is positive. What is $g(2)$?

- **A.** $12$
- **B.** $15$
- **C.** $18$
- **D.** $48$

---

<details>
<summary><b>Answers / Teacher Reference</b></summary>

**1. B** — Equal increases in $x$ give a constant **difference** for a linear function and a constant **ratio** for an exponential function:

$$
6-3=9-6=12-9=3
$$

$$
\frac63=\frac{12}{6}=\frac{24}{12}=2
$$

**2. C** — First find the change per unit of $x$:

$$
m=\frac{19-7}{5-2}=4
$$

Then:

$$
f(3.5)=7+4(3.5-2)=\boxed{13}
$$

**3. A** — Across two units of $x$, the output multiplies by $24/6=4$. Therefore, the factor per unit is:

$$
b^2=4\quad\Rightarrow\quad b=2
$$

$$
g(2)=6(2)=\boxed{12}
$$

The midpoint input gives the **arithmetic mean** of the outputs for a linear function, but the **geometric mean** for a positive exponential function.
