# Malta Year 8 Math — Prime Factors Practice

**6 questions:** 3 prime products and 3 using powers.

- Every final factor must be prime.
- Check that the factors multiply back to the original number.
- A power records how many times a prime is repeated.

---

## Prime products

### 1. Prime factorisation of 24

Which is the **complete prime factorisation** of **24**?

- **A.** $2\times12$
- **B.** $2\times2\times2\times3$
- **C.** $2\times2\times3$
- **D.** $2\times3\times3$

### 2. Prime factorisation of 36

Which is the **complete prime factorisation** of **36**?

- **A.** $4\times9$
- **B.** $2\times3\times3$
- **C.** $2\times2\times2\times3$
- **D.** $2\times2\times3\times3$

### 3. Prime factorisation of 42

Which is the **complete prime factorisation** of **42**?

- **A.** $2\times3\times7$
- **B.** $6\times7$
- **C.** $2\times3\times5$
- **D.** $2\times7\times7$

## Index notation

### 4. Prime factorisation of 60

Which is the **complete prime factorisation** of **60**, written using powers?

- **A.** $2\times3\times5$
- **B.** $2^{2}\times3^{2}\times5$
- **C.** $2^{2}\times3\times5$
- **D.** $4\times15$

### 5. Prime factorisation of 84

Which is the **complete prime factorisation** of **84**, written using powers?

- **A.** $2\times3\times7$
- **B.** $2^{2}\times3\times7$
- **C.** $2^{2}\times3^{2}\times7$
- **D.** $4\times21$

### 6. Prime factorisation of 90

Which is the **complete prime factorisation** of **90**, written using powers?

- **A.** $2^{2}\times3\times5$
- **B.** $2\times3\times5$
- **C.** $9\times10$
- **D.** $2\times3^{2}\times5$

**1. B** — Divide by prime numbers until every final factor is prime.

$$
\begin{aligned}
24\div2&=12 \\
12\div2&=6 \\
6\div2&=3 \\
3\div3&=1
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
24=2\times2\times2\times3
$$

$$
24=\boxed{2\times2\times2\times3}
$$

- **Avoid the trap:** every factor must be prime and the product must equal the original number.
- An equivalent product containing a composite factor is not yet prime factorisation.
