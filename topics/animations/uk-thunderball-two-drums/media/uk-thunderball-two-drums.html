Counting the Main Drum

The order of the 5 numbers does not matter, so count combinations.
$\dbinom{39}{5}$
$=\dfrac{39\cdot38\cdot37\cdot36\cdot35}{5\cdot4\cdot3\cdot2\cdot1}$
$=\dfrac{69{,}090{,}840}{120}$
$=575{,}757$
575,757 ways to pick the 5 main numbers.
