# AP Statistics Roadmap

![AP Statistics roadmap: five unit icons with full unit titles](media/ap-roadmap-statistics.svg)

## What happens

1. The purple frame with three dots along its top edge draws itself and the course title appears: AP Statistics, five units.
2. Unit 1, Exploring One-Variable Data and Collecting Data: twelve scattered observations move into four stacks above a number line; no dot is created or lost.
3. Unit 2, Probability, Random Variables, and Probability Distributions: a token follows one path down a two-stage branching tree while probability bars in the ratio $1 : 2 : 1$ rise beneath the three outcomes.
4. Unit 3, Inference for Categorical Data: Proportions: six filled circles out of ten are highlighted together, then a confidence-interval whisker with a central estimate appears below them.
5. Unit 4, Inference for Quantitative Data: Means: a dashed mean line appears at the average height of five measurement rods, and a separate vertical interval whisker is centered on that mean.
6. Unit 5, Regression Analysis: a best-fit line is drawn through six stationary points, then three residual segments appear from points to the line.
7. The road fills in unit by unit and ends as a poster of all five unit icons with their titles.

## Concept

$$
\text{estimate} \;\pm\; (\text{critical value})(\text{standard error})
$$

AP Statistics moves from individual observations to chance to inference to relationships. The crucial distinction in the middle of the course is that proportions count categories while means summarize measured quantities, which is why Unit 3 shows filled and outlined circles and Unit 4 shows rods of different heights. Both end in the same shape of answer: an estimate with an interval of uncertainty around it.

## Summary

The roadmap shows AP Statistics as five units: describing and collecting data, probability, inference for proportions, inference for means, and regression. Each icon shows the one statistical action that defines its unit, from stacking observations to drawing a fitted line with its residuals.
