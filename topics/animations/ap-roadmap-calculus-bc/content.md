# AP Calculus BC Roadmap

![AP Calculus BC roadmap: ten unit icons with full unit titles](media/ap-roadmap-calculus-bc.svg)

## What happens

1. The gold double frame with two upward chevrons draws itself and the course title appears: AP Calculus BC, ten units.
2. Units 1 to 8 replay the AP Calculus AB actions exactly, in gold with the BC frame: the two points approaching an open circle, the secant becoming a tangent, the pulse through chain links, the speedometer, the tangent finding the maximum, the rectangles becoming area, the solution curve through a slope field, and the triangle revolving into a cone.
3. Unit 9, Parametric Equations, Polar Coordinates, and Vector-Valued Functions: a point travels once around an ellipse with a faint position vector from the origin and a tangent arrow that always points along the direction of motion.
4. Unit 10, Infinite Sequences and Series: a bar fills by successive halves, $\tfrac12$ , $\tfrac14$ , $\tfrac18$ , $\tfrac1{16}$ , with the remaining sliver fading toward the right end under an infinity sign.
5. The road fills in unit by unit and ends as a poster of all ten unit icons with their titles.

## Concept

$$
\text{BC} = \text{AB foundation} + \text{parametric, polar, vector, and series topics}
$$

AP Calculus BC contains the whole AB progression and adds two units. Unit 9 describes motion and curves through parameters, polar coordinates, and vector-valued functions, so position and direction live together on one path. Unit 10 asks when infinitely many terms add to a finite total, for example

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{2^k} = 1 .
$$

The shared icons keep the same central geometry as AB and change only the frame, the color, the chevrons, and a brief highlight, so the two courses read as the same abilities with an advanced badge.

## Summary

The roadmap shows AP Calculus BC as the eight AB units plus parametric, polar, and vector-valued functions and infinite series. The gold double frame marks the advanced course while the shared icons keep the common ground with AB visible.
