# AP Calculus AB Roadmap

![AP Calculus AB roadmap: eight unit icons with full unit titles](media/ap-roadmap-calculus-ab.svg)

## What happens

1. The course frame draws itself and the course title appears: AP Calculus AB, eight units.
2. Unit 1, Limits and Continuity: two points approach the same open circle from both sides and stop just short of it.
3. Unit 2, Differentiation: Definition and Fundamental Properties: a second point slides toward a fixed point and the secant through them turns into the tangent.
4. Unit 3, Differentiation: Composite, Implicit, and Inverse Functions: a pulse passes through two interlocking chain links.
5. Unit 4, Contextual Applications of Differentiation: a speedometer needle rises while a dot on the track below speeds up with it.
6. Unit 5, Analytical Applications of Differentiation: a tangent segment climbs a hill, turns horizontal at the peak, descends, and the maximum is ringed.
7. Unit 6, Integration and Accumulation of Change: four, then eight, then sixteen rectangles under a curve resolve into the filled area.
8. Unit 7, Differential Equations: a solution curve grows from an initial point through a slope field for $y' = y$ .
9. Unit 8, Applications of Integration: a triangle rotates about the axis into a cone with three circular cross-sections.
10. The road fills in unit by unit and ends as a poster of all eight unit icons with their titles.

## Concept

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\int_a^x f(t)\,dt = f(x)
$$

AP Calculus AB follows one path: limits define the derivative, the derivative is applied in context and in analysis, and integration accumulates change and measures area and volume. The three differentiation units are deliberately distinct in the icons: Unit 2 shows how a derivative is defined, Unit 4 shows its physical meaning as a rate, and Unit 5 shows how it reveals the shape of a graph.

## Summary

The roadmap shows AP Calculus AB as eight units from limits through derivatives to integrals and their applications. Each icon carries one recognizable mathematical action, so a student can place any topic on the road before reading its title.
