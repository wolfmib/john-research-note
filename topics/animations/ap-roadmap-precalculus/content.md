# AP Precalculus Roadmap

![AP Precalculus roadmap: four unit icons with full unit titles](media/ap-roadmap-precalculus.svg)

## What happens

1. The course frame draws itself and the course title appears: AP Precalculus, four units.
2. Unit 1, Polynomial and Rational Functions: a parabola and two disconnected rational branches are traced beside a vertical asymptote.
3. Unit 2, Exponential and Logarithmic Functions: an exponential curve is reflected across the line $y = x$ into its logarithmic inverse, and two dots trade horizontal and vertical positions.
4. Unit 3, Trigonometric and Polar Functions: a radius turns once around a circle while its height traces one cycle of a sine wave.
5. Unit 4, Functions Involving Parameters, Vectors, and Matrices: one basis vector stays fixed while the other shears, and the parallelogram between them changes with it.
6. The road at the bottom fills in unit by unit and ends as a poster of all four unit icons with their titles.

## Concept

$$
f(x) \;\longrightarrow\; \text{behavior, transformations, applications}
$$

AP Precalculus is organized around function models. Each unit is one family of functions and the behavior that family can describe: polynomial and rational growth with asymptotes, exponential growth with its logarithmic inverse, periodic behavior generated by rotation, and parameter, vector, and matrix descriptions of change. Units 1 to 3 are assessed on the AP Exam; Unit 4 is part of the course but not assessed.

## Summary

The roadmap shows AP Precalculus as four function families in order: polynomial and rational, exponential and logarithmic, trigonometric and polar, and finally parameters, vectors, and matrices. Each icon shows the one idea a student should recognize before reading the unit title.
