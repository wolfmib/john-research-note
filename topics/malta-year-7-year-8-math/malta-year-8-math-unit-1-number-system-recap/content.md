# Malta Year 8 Math Unit 1 — Number System Recap

Two questions for each subtopic, 1-1 to 1-4. **Show your method before choosing A–D.**

---

## 1. 1-1 · Value of a power

What is the value of $4^3$?

- **A.** 12
- **B.** 81
- **C.** 64
- **D.** 16

## 2. 1-1 · Powers in BIDMAS

Calculate $5 + 2\times3^3$.

- **A.** 189
- **B.** 59
- **C.** 221
- **D.** 23

## 3. 1-2 · LCM of three numbers

What is the LCM of 6, 10 and 15?

- **A.** 60
- **B.** 900
- **C.** 15
- **D.** 30

## 4. 1-2 · Lights flashing together

Three lights flash every 4, 6 and 10 seconds. They flash together now. After how many seconds do they next flash together?

- **A.** 20
- **B.** 120
- **C.** 60
- **D.** 240

## 5. 1-3 · Square and cube roots

Which pair gives $\sqrt{225}$ and $\sqrt[3]{343}$?

- **A.** 15 and 7
- **B.** 112.5 and 7
- **C.** 15 and 49
- **D.** 25 and 7

## 6. 1-3 · Estimating a root

Between which two whole numbers does $\sqrt{90}$ lie?

- **A.** 8 and 9
- **B.** 9 and 10
- **C.** 44 and 46
- **D.** 10 and 11

## 7. 1-4 · Terminating or recurring

Which fraction gives a recurring decimal?

- **A.** $\frac38$
- **B.** $\frac{7}{25}$
- **C.** $\frac{9}{20}$
- **D.** $\frac{5}{12}$

## 8. 1-4 · Recurring decimal to fraction

Which fraction is exactly equal to $0.\dot{5}$?

- **A.** $\frac59$
- **B.** $\frac12$
- **C.** $\frac{11}{20}$
- **D.** $\frac{5}{99}$

---

<details>
<summary><b>Answers</b></summary>

**1. C — Three fours multiplied**

$$
4^3 = 4\times4\times4 = \boxed{64}
$$

12 multiplies the base by the exponent; 81 is $3^4$, with base and exponent swapped; 16 is $4^2$.

**2. B — Power, then multiply, then add**

$$
5 + 2\times27 = 5 + 54 = \boxed{59}
$$

189 adds first, $(5 + 2)\times27$; 221 cubes $2\times3$ first; 23 reads $3^3$ as 9.

**3. D — Every prime at its highest power**

$$
\begin{aligned} 6 &= 2\times3 \\ 10 &= 2\times5 \\ 15 &= 3\times5 \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\text{LCM} = 2\times3\times5 = \boxed{30}
$$

60 is a common multiple but not the lowest; 900 is the product $6\times10\times15$; 15 is not a multiple of 6.

**4. C — LCM of 4, 6 and 10**

$$
\begin{aligned} 4 &= 2^2 \\ 6 &= 2\times3 \\ 10 &= 2\times5 \end{aligned}
$$

$$
\text{LCM} = 2^2\times3\times5 = \boxed{60}
$$

20 adds the times; 240 is the product $4\times6\times10$; 120 is a common multiple, but 60 comes first.

**5. A — Undo the square and the cube**

$15\times15 = 225$ and $7\times7\times7 = 343$, so the pair is 15 and 7.

112.5 halves 225 — a root is not a division by 2; 49 is $7^2$, not the cube root.

**6. B — Perfect squares either side**

$$
81 < 90 < 100 \quad\Rightarrow\quad 9 < \sqrt{90} < 10
$$

44 and 46 sit around $90\div2 = 45$ — halving is not taking a root.

**7. D — A prime factor other than 2 or 5**

$\frac{5}{12}$ is in simplest form and $12 = 2^2\times3$; the 3 makes it recur: $\frac{5}{12} = 0.41\dot{6}$.

The others terminate: $\frac38 = 0.375$, $\frac{7}{25} = 0.28$, $\frac{9}{20} = 0.45$.

**8. A — One repeating digit over 9**

$0.\dot{5} = 0.555\ldots = \frac59$.

$\frac12 = 0.5$ and $\frac{11}{20} = 0.55$ stop; $\frac{5}{99} = 0.\dot{0}\dot{5} = 0.0505\ldots$

A power repeats multiplication, an LCM takes every prime at its highest power, a root undoes a power, and a fraction in simplest form terminates only when its denominator has no prime factors other than 2 and 5.

</details>
