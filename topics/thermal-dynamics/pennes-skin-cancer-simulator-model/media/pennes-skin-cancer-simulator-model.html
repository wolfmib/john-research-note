The Scene

A 48 × 48 mm patch of skin with a lesion, heated by a stimulator pad on the surface.

Pennes Bioheat Equation

The heat balance of every point in the tissue, with the conditions on its faces.

Rest State

Before the stimulus the skin is in equilibrium with the room and the core: $\partial T/\partial t = 0$.

Heating: 90 s

The stimulator adds a heat flux on $\Sigma$; convection still acts on the whole surface.

Recovery: 300 s

The stimulator is off. The skin cools back towards its rest state.

Five skin layers down to the core at 11.7 mm (layer values: Kandala, Deng & Herman 2013).
The lesion fills the top 1.0 mm: the epidermis, the papillary dermis and 0.1 mm of the reticular dermis.
Conduction moves heat from warm to cold, sideways and in depth.
Blood at $T_b$ = 37 °C exchanges heat with the tissue; $m(x,y)$ is a smooth random perfusion multiplier (log-normal, $\sigma$ = 0.15, 1 mm). Darker red = more blood flow.
Metabolic heat $Q_m$. The lesion has about × the blood perfusion and × the metabolic heat of the reticular dermis.
Convection to the 21 °C room acts on the whole surface; the stimulator flux $q(t)$ only on $\Sigma$. The bottom is held at 37 °C; the sides are insulated.
The lesion is warmer at rest: more blood flow and more metabolic heat.
